AI Productivity at a glance

The AI Productivity Academy is a set of 10 EduCut.ai courses (A004.01–A004.10) designed for business professionals who want measurable productivity gains from AI in their everyday work. It covers Email & Communication, Documents, Meetings, Research, Excel & Data and Automation, from foundation to advanced level. Each course takes 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work) and combines instructor-led online sessions with self-paced personal work.

Courses in the AI Productivity Academy

10 courses, from foundation to advanced level. Open a course to see its programme.

Foundation level · 3 courses

A004.01

AI Productivity Fundamentals

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand how generative AI can support everyday professional activities and where it can improve speed, quality, and consistency.
  • Identify workplace tasks that are suitable for AI assistance, including writing, summarization, analysis, planning, research, and information organization.
  • Select appropriate AI tools according to the task, expected output, available information, and professional context.
  • Develop effective human-AI working habits that combine AI assistance with human judgment, verification, and accountability.
A004.02

AI for Email & Professional Communication

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to draft, revise, and respond to professional emails while preserving the intended message and communication objective.
  • Adapt tone, style, length, level of formality, and language according to different audiences and professional situations.
  • Summarize email threads and conversations to identify key information, decisions, requests, deadlines, and follow-up actions.
  • Create reusable AI-assisted communication workflows and templates for recurring professional correspondence.
A004.03

AI for Documents, Reports & Presentations

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to create and improve reports, proposals, executive summaries, briefs, and other professional documents.
  • Transform rough ideas, notes, and source materials into coherent structures with appropriate sections, arguments, and supporting information.
  • Generate presentation outlines, slide content, key messages, and speaker notes adapted to a specific audience and purpose.
  • Review AI-generated documents for clarity, consistency, accuracy, tone, structure, and overall professional quality.

Intermediate level · 4 courses

A004.04

AI for Meetings & Team Collaboration

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to prepare meeting agendas, discussion questions, briefing materials, and background information before collaborative sessions.
  • Transform meeting notes or transcripts into concise summaries that capture decisions, responsibilities, action items, and deadlines.
  • Apply AI-assisted brainstorming techniques to generate, organize, compare, and refine ideas during collaborative problem-solving.
  • Design workflows that automate meeting follow-ups, distribute knowledge, and help teams maintain continuity between discussions.
A004.05

AI for Research & Information Synthesis

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to explore, organize, and structure information collected from multiple documents or professional sources.
  • Summarize long or complex materials while preserving the main arguments, evidence, conclusions, and important contextual details.
  • Compare sources, viewpoints, approaches, products, or alternatives using explicit criteria and structured analytical frameworks.
  • Transform complex information into research briefs, comparison matrices, executive summaries, and actionable recommendations.
A004.06

AI for Excel, Data Analysis & Visualization

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to generate, explain, troubleshoot, and improve spreadsheet formulas for common business analysis tasks.
  • Support the cleaning, transformation, categorization, and exploration of business datasets using AI-assisted methods.
  • Identify trends, patterns, anomalies, relationships, and relevant key performance indicators while distinguishing evidence from interpretation.
  • Create appropriate visualizations and use AI to communicate data-driven findings through concise explanations, summaries, and decision-oriented insights.

A004.07

AI for Planning, Projects & Decision-Making

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Transform strategic objectives and business requirements into structured project plans, milestones, tasks, responsibilities, and deliverables.
  • Use AI to prioritize activities and identify dependencies, risks, resource constraints, bottlenecks, and potential implementation challenges.
  • Compare alternatives and explore scenarios by defining decision criteria, assumptions, trade-offs, opportunities, and possible consequences.
  • Use structured AI-assisted analysis to support professional decision-making while retaining human responsibility for final judgments.

Advanced level · 3 courses

A004.08

Building Personal AI Workflows

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Identify repetitive or time-consuming professional tasks that can be redesigned through systematic use of AI.
  • Combine prompts, reusable templates, documents, contextual information, and AI tools into repeatable workflows for specific tasks.
  • Create personalized AI working systems adapted to an individual's role, responsibilities, recurring activities, and preferred outputs.
  • Reduce manual effort while introducing verification steps, quality standards, and human review to maintain reliable and consistent results.
A004.09

AI Automation & No-Code Workflows

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand the principles of AI-enabled automation and how information can move between AI systems and common business applications.
  • Design trigger-based workflows that initiate actions automatically when defined events, conditions, or inputs occur.
  • Connect AI capabilities with productivity and no-code tools to automate repetitive activities such as classification, summarization, routing, drafting, and reporting.
  • Develop end-to-end automation scenarios for realistic workplace processes while incorporating validation, exception handling, and human oversight where necessary.
A004.10

Building Your AI-Powered Workplace

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Identify and prioritize high-impact opportunities for AI adoption across teams, functions, and organizational processes.
  • Redesign workflows around effective human-AI collaboration by determining which activities should be automated, augmented, reviewed, or retained by people.
  • Address privacy, responsible AI use, governance, security, adoption, and change-management considerations when introducing AI into workplace processes.
  • Develop an AI transformation roadmap with priorities, implementation stages, success indicators, and methods for measuring productivity gains and business impact.
