Academy A007 · at a glance

AI for Marketing at a glance

The AI for Marketing Academy is a set of 10 EduCut.ai courses (A007.01–A007.10) designed for marketing, communication, acquisition and customer-experience professionals. It covers Strategy, Content, Social Media, SEO, Advertising and Analytics & Automation, from foundation to advanced level. Each course takes 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work) and combines instructor-led online sessions with self-paced personal work.

Courses in the AI for Marketing Academy

10 courses, from foundation to advanced level. Open a course to see its programme.

Foundation level · 2 courses

A007.01

AI Fundamentals for Marketing

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand how generative AI and predictive AI are transforming modern marketing practices, capabilities, and customer engagement.
  • Explore major AI applications across the customer journey and marketing funnel, from awareness and acquisition to conversion, retention, and loyalty.
  • Identify marketing activities that can be augmented or automated with AI while determining where human creativity, judgment, and oversight remain essential.
  • Build the conceptual and practical foundations required to integrate AI responsibly into everyday marketing activities.
A007.02

AI for Marketing Strategy & Planning

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to analyze markets, competitors, emerging trends, customer expectations, and changing business conditions.
  • Develop and refine customer personas, positioning statements, value propositions, marketing objectives, and strategic messaging with AI assistance.
  • Generate, compare, and evaluate campaign concepts, market-entry ideas, and go-to-market strategies using structured AI-supported analysis.
  • Translate business objectives into coherent AI-assisted marketing plans with priorities, audiences, channels, activities, and measurable outcomes.

Intermediate level · 5 courses

A007.03

AI for Content Marketing & Copywriting

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to develop blogs, landing pages, newsletters, product descriptions, campaign copy, and other marketing content from structured briefs.
  • Adapt content to different customer segments, communication channels, tones of voice, stages of the customer journey, and organizational brand guidelines.
  • Repurpose existing material efficiently across formats and channels while preserving the original message and strategic intent.
  • Establish human review and editorial workflows that protect originality, factual accuracy, quality, legal compliance, and brand consistency.
A007.04

AI for Social Media Marketing

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Generate platform-specific social media concepts, captions, hooks, calls to action, campaign ideas, and creative variations with AI.
  • Build AI-assisted content calendars and publishing strategies aligned with campaign objectives, audience behavior, and channel requirements.
  • Analyze audience engagement, social conversations, feedback, and emerging trends to identify opportunities for relevant and timely communication.
  • Use AI-supported insights to strengthen community engagement and continuously improve social media performance.
A007.05

AI for SEO & Digital Visibility

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to support keyword research, search-intent analysis, topic discovery, content clustering, and SEO-oriented editorial planning.
  • Create and optimize search-focused content while maintaining usefulness, originality, relevance, readability, and alignment with audience needs.
  • Analyze competitors, content gaps, search themes, and opportunities that can contribute to sustainable organic visibility and growth.
  • Understand how generative and AI-powered search experiences are changing traditional SEO practices, content discovery, and online visibility.
A007.06

AI for Advertising & Campaign Optimization

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to develop campaign concepts, advertising copy, creative variations, audience hypotheses, and messaging alternatives across paid channels.
  • Apply AI to support audience targeting, segmentation, experimentation, and A/B testing while maintaining appropriate human oversight.
  • Analyze campaign results to identify performance patterns, weaknesses, opportunities, and potential optimization actions.
  • Connect AI-generated insights with marketing KPIs and business objectives to improve advertising efficiency and campaign effectiveness.
A007.07

AI for Customer Insights & Personalization

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to analyze customer data, reviews, survey responses, feedback, conversations, and behavioral signals in order to identify meaningful insights.
  • Develop customer segments and uncover recurring needs, preferences, motivations, frustrations, and pain points.
  • Design personalized messages, offers, recommendations, content, and customer experiences adapted to relevant customer contexts and lifecycle stages.
  • Balance personalization opportunities with privacy, transparency, consent, fairness, and responsible use of customer data.

Advanced level · 3 courses

A007.08

AI for Marketing Analytics & Performance

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to interpret marketing datasets, dashboards, funnels, channel metrics, and campaign results through natural-language analytical workflows.
  • Identify trends, anomalies, conversion opportunities, performance drivers, and potential causes of changes in marketing outcomes.
  • Generate actionable insights from complex marketing information while distinguishing observed evidence from assumptions and AI-generated interpretation.
  • Transform analytical findings into clear recommendations and decision-oriented narratives for marketing teams, managers, and executive stakeholders.
A007.09

AI Marketing Automation & Customer Journeys

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Design AI-powered workflows across email marketing, CRM systems, lead nurturing, customer engagement, and lifecycle management.
  • Automate repetitive marketing activities while defining appropriate checkpoints for human review, approval, exception handling, and quality control.
  • Build personalized customer journeys that adapt communications and actions according to behaviors, segments, interactions, and lifecycle stages.
  • Connect AI capabilities, automation tools, customer data, and marketing platforms into scalable end-to-end workflows.
A007.10

Building an AI-Powered Marketing Strategy

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Integrate AI strategically across content marketing, social media, SEO, advertising, customer insights, analytics, personalization, and automation.
  • Identify, evaluate, and prioritize high-value AI marketing use cases according to expected impact, feasibility, cost, risk, and organizational readiness.
  • Define KPIs, governance mechanisms, workflows, responsibilities, review processes, and measurement frameworks for responsible AI-enabled marketing.
  • Develop a complete AI marketing transformation roadmap focused on sustainable growth, operational efficiency, customer value, and measurable return on investment.
