AI for Education at a glance

The AI for Education Academy is a set of 10 EduCut.ai courses (A010.01–A010.10) designed for teachers, trainers, academic leaders, researchers and education professionals. It covers Course Design, Teaching, Personalized Learning, Assessment, Content Creation and Academic Integrity, from foundation to advanced level. Each course takes 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work) and combines instructor-led online sessions with self-paced personal work.

Courses in the AI for Education Academy

10 courses, from foundation to advanced level. Open a course to see its programme.

Foundation level · 2 courses

A010.01

AI Fundamentals for Education

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand artificial intelligence, generative AI, large language models, and their evolving role across modern educational environments.
  • Explore practical opportunities for AI across teaching, learning, assessment, student support, research, and academic administration.
  • Identify educational activities that can be enhanced through AI while recognizing where educator expertise, pedagogical judgment, and human interaction remain essential.
  • Understand the capabilities, limitations, risks, and principles of responsible AI use in schools, universities, training organizations, and other learning environments.
A010.02

AI for Lesson & Course Design

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to develop learning objectives, lesson plans, course structures, teaching sequences, and instructional materials aligned with defined educational goals.
  • Align AI-generated learning content with curriculum requirements, competency frameworks, assessment expectations, and intended learning outcomes.
  • Create differentiated activities, explanations, exercises, and learning resources adapted to different learner levels, abilities, and educational needs.
  • Accelerate course preparation and instructional design while maintaining pedagogical coherence, content quality, and meaningful instructor oversight.

Intermediate level · 5 courses

A010.03

AI for Teaching & Classroom Engagement

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Integrate AI appropriately into lectures, classroom activities, discussions, workshops, and collaborative learning experiences.
  • Generate examples, explanations, case studies, simulations, discussion prompts, and interactive exercises that reinforce specific learning objectives.
  • Use AI to explain difficult concepts through alternative representations, examples, analogies, and levels of complexity adapted to different learners.
  • Design engaging human-AI learning experiences that encourage active participation, questioning, reflection, collaboration, and critical thinking.
A010.04

AI for Personalized & Adaptive Learning

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to personalize educational content and learning support according to learner knowledge, progress, objectives, performance, and individual needs.
  • Create differentiated learning pathways, remediation activities, enrichment opportunities, and targeted support for learners with different competency profiles.
  • Explore AI tutors, adaptive learning systems, personalized recommendations, and feedback mechanisms that can support individualized learning experiences.
  • Balance personalization with learner autonomy, fairness, transparency, accessibility, data protection, and appropriate teacher supervision.
A010.05

AI for Assessment & Feedback

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to support the creation of quizzes, assignments, rubrics, examinations, formative assessments, and other evaluation activities aligned with learning outcomes.
  • Generate personalized, constructive, and actionable feedback that helps learners understand their performance and identify concrete areas for improvement.
  • Analyze student responses and learning evidence to identify misconceptions, knowledge gaps, recurring difficulties, and patterns of learning progress.
  • Maintain fairness, reliability, transparency, assessment validity, and meaningful educator oversight whenever AI contributes to evaluation or feedback.
A010.06

AI for Educational Content Creation

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Create educational texts, presentations, exercises, case studies, visual materials, examples, and multimedia learning resources with AI assistance.
  • Adapt existing educational materials for different ages, competency levels, languages, accessibility requirements, and learning contexts.
  • Transform complex or technical information into accessible, structured, and engaging educational content without losing essential meaning.
  • Evaluate AI-generated educational materials for factual accuracy, quality, inclusiveness, accessibility, bias, copyright considerations, and pedagogical relevance.
A010.07

AI for Research & Academic Productivity

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to support literature exploration, academic reading, information organization, research planning, and knowledge synthesis.
  • Summarize and compare scholarly materials while identifying recurring themes, methodological differences, arguments, findings, limitations, and potential research gaps.
  • Improve academic workflows involving writing support, data exploration, note organization, document analysis, and research knowledge management.
  • Apply responsible practices for verification, attribution, citation awareness, confidentiality, and academic integrity when using AI in research activities.

Advanced level · 3 courses

A010.08

AI for Student Support & Educational Administration

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to improve student guidance, frequently asked questions, academic support, service navigation, and access to institutional information.
  • Automate appropriate repetitive administrative activities involving communication, scheduling, documentation, information retrieval, and routine service requests.
  • Design AI assistants that help students, faculty, and staff access approved institutional knowledge and services more efficiently.
  • Improve operational efficiency while maintaining privacy, accessibility, information accuracy, escalation mechanisms, and meaningful human support for complex or sensitive situations.
A010.09

Academic Integrity, Ethics & Responsible AI

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Address academic and ethical challenges associated with plagiarism, AI-generated assignments, hallucinations, bias, privacy, copyright, authorship, and responsible AI use.
  • Redesign assessment approaches for educational environments in which learners have routine access to generative AI and other intelligent tools.
  • Develop transparent institutional or course-level policies that define acceptable, restricted, and unacceptable uses of AI in teaching, learning, assessment, and research.
  • Promote AI literacy, verification, critical thinking, transparency, responsible authorship, and sound academic practices among educators and learners.

A010.10

Building an AI-Powered Education Strategy

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Identify and prioritize high-impact AI opportunities across teaching, learning, assessment, research, student services, and educational administration.
  • Evaluate institutional readiness across leadership, people, skills, technology, data, policies, governance, infrastructure, and pedagogical practices.
  • Define governance structures, professional development, adoption mechanisms, evaluation methods, responsibilities, and success frameworks for educational AI initiatives.
  • Develop a practical roadmap for responsible, inclusive, measurable, and sustainable AI transformation across an educational institution.
