Questions about the EduCut.ai courses

How many courses does EduCut.ai offer?

212 courses, organized into 18 specialized academies — from AI foundations and generative AI to AI for business functions, LLM engineering, RAG, AI agents, responsible AI, governance, security and transformation.

What levels are the courses?

Courses are offered at foundation (53 courses), intermediate (106 courses) and advanced (53 courses) level.

How long is a course?

150 courses take 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work) and 62 courses take 12 hours (8 h online + 4 h personal work).

How are the courses delivered?

Blended: instructor-led online sessions combined with self-paced personal work.

Do the courses include a certificate?

Yes. Certificate awarded upon completion.

In which language are the courses taught?

English, and translation is available.
