AI for HR at a glance

The AI for HR Academy is a set of 10 EduCut.ai courses (A009.01–A009.10) designed for HR professionals, talent leaders, L&D teams, people analytics professionals and managers. It covers Recruitment, Onboarding, L&D, Performance, People Analytics and Workforce Planning, from foundation to advanced level. Each course takes 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work) and combines instructor-led online sessions with self-paced personal work.

Courses in the AI for HR Academy

10 courses, from foundation to advanced level. Open a course to see its programme.

Foundation level · 2 courses

A009.01

AI Fundamentals for Human Resources

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand how artificial intelligence and generative AI are transforming modern human resources and changing how HR teams deliver services, support employees, and make workforce decisions.
  • Explore practical AI applications across recruitment, talent management, learning and development, employee engagement, workforce planning, and HR operations.
  • Identify HR activities that can be augmented or automated with AI while recognizing where empathy, professional judgment, and human accountability remain essential.
  • Understand the opportunities, limitations, organizational implications, and human consequences associated with adopting AI across the employee lifecycle.
A009.02

AI for Recruitment & Talent Acquisition

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to develop job descriptions, sourcing strategies, interview questions, candidate communications, and other recruitment materials aligned with role requirements.
  • Explore AI-assisted candidate screening, matching, sourcing, and recruitment workflows while understanding the appropriate boundaries of automated support.
  • Improve consistency, efficiency, and information management throughout the hiring process without removing meaningful recruiter and hiring-manager judgment.
  • Recognize and mitigate risks related to bias, discrimination, unfair screening, opacity, and inappropriate automated decision-making in recruitment.

Intermediate level · 5 courses

A009.03

AI for Employee Onboarding & Experience

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Design personalized onboarding journeys supported by AI assistants, adaptive guidance, and intelligent content tailored to employee roles and needs.
  • Automate repetitive employee questions, documentation requests, administrative interactions, and routine onboarding communications where appropriate.
  • Use AI to improve employee access to organizational knowledge, policies, procedures, benefits information, and HR services.
  • Create more responsive and personalized employee experiences across onboarding and the broader employment lifecycle while maintaining appropriate human support.
A009.04

AI for Learning, Training & Skills Development

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI-assisted analysis to identify current skill gaps, emerging capability requirements, and development priorities across individuals, teams, and the organization.
  • Create personalized learning paths, training materials, assessments, exercises, and learning activities adapted to different roles and competency levels.
  • Recommend relevant development opportunities based on employee roles, existing skills, career objectives, and evolving organizational requirements.
  • Build continuous learning and reskilling strategies that prepare employees for changing responsibilities and AI-driven workplace transformation.
A009.05

AI for Performance & Talent Management

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to support goal setting, performance-review preparation, competency analysis, feedback organization, and employee development planning.
  • Analyze relevant performance information to identify strengths, development needs, recurring patterns, and potential talent opportunities.
  • Support managers with more structured, evidence-informed preparation for performance and development conversations while avoiding automated judgments about employees.
  • Maintain meaningful human judgment, contextual interpretation, transparency, and managerial accountability whenever AI contributes to employee-related decisions.
A009.06

AI for Employee Engagement & People Analytics

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to analyze employee surveys, qualitative feedback, workforce information, and other organizational signals to better understand engagement and employee experience.
  • Identify patterns related to satisfaction, collaboration, organizational sentiment, retention, workplace concerns, and other relevant people indicators.
  • Transform HR and workforce data into meaningful dashboards, summaries, insights, and recommendations for HR and organizational leaders.
  • Apply people analytics responsibly by protecting employee privacy, confidentiality, data minimization, and appropriate limits on workforce monitoring.
A009.07

AI for Workforce Planning & Skills Intelligence

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to analyze workforce capacity, skills inventories, organizational roles, talent supply, and future capability requirements.
  • Identify emerging skill gaps and examine how automation and AI augmentation may change existing jobs, tasks, and competency requirements.
  • Support headcount planning, succession, internal mobility, workforce development, and reskilling decisions with structured data-driven insights.
  • Develop workforce scenarios aligned with future organizational strategy, expected technological change, and evolving talent requirements.

Advanced level · 3 courses

A009.08

AI for HR Productivity & Automation

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Automate repetitive HR activities such as documentation, employee requests, reporting, internal communications, information classification, and routine administrative work.
  • Design AI-assisted workflows that connect HR systems, organizational documents, knowledge sources, and workplace applications.
  • Build reusable HR assistants and workflows that support common employee and manager needs while providing consistent access to approved information.
  • Reduce administrative workload while preserving human review, exception handling, accountability, data protection, and appropriate escalation mechanisms.
A009.09

Responsible AI, Ethics & Governance in HR

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand bias, fairness, transparency, privacy, explainability, accountability, and human oversight requirements in AI-supported HR processes.
  • Identify high-risk AI applications involving recruitment, employee evaluation, promotion, workforce monitoring, disciplinary processes, and other consequential employment decisions.
  • Establish governance principles, decision rights, review procedures, documentation requirements, and human oversight mechanisms for responsible AI use in HR.
  • Balance innovation and efficiency with employee rights, organizational trust, regulatory requirements, ethical responsibilities, and the organization's values.

See also: Responsible AI courses →

A009.10

Building an AI-Powered HR Strategy

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Identify and prioritize high-value AI opportunities across recruitment, onboarding, learning, performance, engagement, workforce planning, and HR operations.
  • Integrate AI initiatives across the complete employee lifecycle while ensuring that technology supports rather than undermines human-centered HR practices.
  • Define adoption plans, governance structures, responsibilities, KPIs, implementation priorities, and success measures for AI-enabled HR transformation.
  • Develop a practical AI transformation roadmap for building a more strategic, efficient, data-informed, responsible, and human-centered HR function.
