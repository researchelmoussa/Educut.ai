AI for Finance at a glance

The AI for Finance Academy is a set of 10 EduCut.ai courses (A008.01–A008.10) designed for finance professionals, analysts, FP&A teams, controllers and finance leaders. It covers Financial Analysis, Forecasting, Accounting, Financial Modeling, Risk and FP&A & Automation, from foundation to advanced level. Each course takes 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work) and combines instructor-led online sessions with self-paced personal work.

Courses in the AI for Finance Academy

10 courses, from foundation to advanced level. Open a course to see its programme.

Foundation level · 2 courses

A008.01

AI Fundamentals for Finance

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Understand how artificial intelligence, machine learning, and generative AI are transforming modern finance and changing the way financial professionals analyze information, execute processes, and support business decisions.
  • Explore practical AI applications across accounting, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), banking, investment activities, and corporate finance.
  • Identify financial activities that can be augmented or automated with AI while recognizing tasks that continue to require professional judgment, validation, and accountability.
  • Understand the opportunities, limitations, and risks associated with applying AI to financial information, particularly where accuracy, confidentiality, traceability, and decision quality are critical.
A008.02

AI for Financial Analysis & Reporting

Foundation · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to support the analysis of income statements, balance sheets, cash-flow statements, management accounts, and other financial reports.
  • Extract relevant financial indicators and use AI-assisted analysis to identify trends, relationships, anomalies, and significant changes in financial performance.
  • Transform complex financial data into concise management summaries, financial narratives, explanations, and performance commentaries.
  • Communicate financial information through clear, decision-oriented insights adapted to executives, managers, investors, and other organizational stakeholders.

Intermediate level · 5 courses

A008.03

AI for Budgeting & Forecasting

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Apply AI-assisted methods to budgeting, revenue forecasting, expense planning, cash-flow projections, and recurring financial planning activities.
  • Build and compare financial scenarios by combining historical information, operational drivers, assumptions, and alternative business conditions.
  • Identify and explain deviations between actual and expected financial performance and use AI to support variance investigation.
  • Use AI-assisted forecasting outputs as decision-support inputs while preserving human validation of assumptions, uncertainty, and business context.
A008.04

AI for Accounting & Financial Operations

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to streamline invoice processing, expense management, reconciliations, transaction classification, and other repetitive accounting and financial operations.
  • Extract structured financial information from invoices, receipts, reports, contracts, and other document-based or unstructured sources.
  • Identify opportunities for automation across routine finance processes and determine where AI can reduce manual work, delays, and operational friction.
  • Design human-in-the-loop workflows that maintain accuracy, traceability, approval requirements, segregation of duties, and appropriate financial controls.
A008.05

AI for Excel & Financial Modeling

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to generate, explain, troubleshoot, validate, and optimize spreadsheet formulas and components of financial models.
  • Accelerate data cleaning, transformation, calculation, reconciliation, and exploratory financial analysis within spreadsheet-based workflows.
  • Develop scenario, sensitivity, profitability, break-even, and cash-flow analyses more efficiently with AI assistance.
  • Apply AI while maintaining explicit assumptions, transparent calculation logic, model documentation, and the auditability required for professional financial models.
A008.06

AI for Risk Management & Fraud Detection

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Explore how AI can identify unusual transactions, behavioral patterns, anomalies, and indicators associated with potential financial fraud.
  • Apply AI-assisted approaches to credit, operational, liquidity, and broader financial risk analysis using relevant financial and business information.
  • Use scenario analysis, monitoring techniques, and early-warning indicators to identify and investigate emerging financial risks.
  • Understand why explainability, validation, governance, and human oversight are particularly important when AI contributes to high-stakes financial or risk-related decisions.
A008.07

AI for Investment & Market Analysis

Intermediate · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Use AI to organize and analyze information about companies, industries, financial performance, and relevant market developments.
  • Extract and synthesize insights from earnings reports, financial disclosures, market news, and large volumes of investment-related information.
  • Compare companies, financial indicators, market scenarios, and investment alternatives using structured analytical criteria.
  • Recognize the uncertainty, hallucination risks, data limitations, and forecasting limitations associated with AI-generated investment analysis and predictions.

Advanced level · 3 courses

A008.08

AI for FP&A & Strategic Decision-Making

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Integrate AI into Financial Planning & Analysis workflows to strengthen business planning, performance management, and strategic decision support.
  • Analyze profitability, margins, cost structures, operational drivers, and financial performance across products, services, departments, or business units.
  • Develop what-if analyses and structured scenarios to evaluate the potential financial consequences of strategic choices and changing business conditions.
  • Translate financial analysis into concise, actionable recommendations that support senior management in evaluating priorities, trade-offs, and strategic options.
A008.09

AI Automation for Finance Teams

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Design AI-powered workflows that connect spreadsheets, financial systems, business documents, and other organizational applications.
  • Automate recurring reporting, information extraction, reconciliation, monitoring, classification, and analytical activities where automation can deliver reliable efficiency gains.
  • Introduce AI agents and workflow automation into appropriate finance processes while clearly defining their scope, permissions, and expected actions.
  • Build scalable automation workflows that preserve approvals, controls, traceability, auditability, exception handling, and meaningful human oversight.
A008.10

AI Governance & Transformation for Finance

Advanced · 9 hours (6 h online + 3 h personal work)
Programme
  • Develop a structured roadmap for integrating AI across finance functions while protecting sensitive financial, customer, employee, and organizational information.
  • Address governance, regulatory compliance, model risk, privacy, cybersecurity, explainability, accountability, and control requirements associated with financial AI use.
  • Define KPIs for measuring productivity improvement, cost reduction, processing speed, analytical quality, accuracy, risk reduction, and broader business value.
  • Design a responsible AI operating model that defines ownership, policies, controls, capabilities, and transformation priorities for scaling AI across the finance function.
