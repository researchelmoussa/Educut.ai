Structure

Aggregated statistics vs. gold (all 887 snippets)

% predicted vs gold = total articles the method predicted ÷ total gold articles (1948). % correct over gold = predicted articles that are also in that snippet's gold label ÷ total gold articles (micro recall). Precision = correct ÷ predicted. Exact match = the predicted set equals the gold set.

Per-article statistics: the 23 GDPR articles in the prompt, plus Class 0 (“no violation detected”)

For each article: how many snippets have it in the gold label, and how many times each method predicted it (the number of correct predictions, meaning the article is also in that snippet's gold label, is shown after the ✓). Articles are ranked by the total number of times they were predicted across the 6 methods. Hover a heatmap cell for details. Class 0 (“no violation detected”) is the answer "0": its row counts the snippets where a method answered 0. No gold label contains it, so it is never correct. It adds no penalty of its own: the gold articles of those snippets are the omissions. It is shown yellow in the omission heatmap, never orange.

Score per snippet: omission vs. hallucination, by method

For each snippet, every gold article the method missed is an omission (−1), every predicted article not in gold is a hallucination (+1), and every correctly predicted article counts 0. Score = hallucinations − omissions. Example: gold = {4, 5}, method = {7} → 2 omissions + 1 hallucination = −1. An answer of 0 is Class 0 (“no violation detected”): the method predicts nothing, so it has 0 predictions and 0 hallucinations, and every gold article of that snippet is an omission. Example: gold = [5], method = 0 → omission −1, prediction 0, hallucination 0 → score −1. Bars: yellow below zero = omitted articles; blue above zero = correct articles (0 points, shown for context); orange stacked on top = hallucinated articles; black dot = net score. All 6 charts share the x axis: zoom or drag the slider at the bottom to move them together.

Hallucinations per article

For each method and each of the 23 articles: the number of times the method predicted that article for a snippet whose gold label does not contain it (hallucination), and below it that article's share of the method's total hallucinations. Class 0 (“no violation detected”) is not listed here: answering 0 predicts nothing, so it cannot be a hallucination.

How to read a cell, e.g. Art. 5 · Baseline · 78 · 18.8%: Baseline hallucinated Art. 5 78 times (it predicted Art. 5 for 78 snippets whose gold label does not contain it), and these 78 make up 18.8% of all Baseline hallucinations (78 ÷ 415). It is not the share of Art. 5 predictions that are wrong: that is the hallucination rate, 78 ÷ 122 = 64% for Baseline.

How to read a cell, e.g. Art. 5 · Baseline · 69 · 18.9%: Baseline hallucinated Art. 5 69 times (it predicted Art. 5 for 69 snippets whose gold label does not contain it), and these 69 make up 18.9% of all Baseline hallucinations (69 ÷ 365). It is not the share of Art. 5 predictions that are wrong: that is the hallucination rate, 69 ÷ 93 = 74% for Baseline.

Overall hallucination per article (all 6 methods)

Hallucinations summed over the 6 methods, with each article classed as High (100 or more hallucinations), Occasional (10–99), Rare (1–9) or Never (0). Share = the article's part of all hallucinations. Hallucination rate = hallucinations ÷ times predicted (how often a prediction of that article is wrong).

How to read a row, e.g. High · Art. 25 · 311 · 1070 · 319 · 751 · 28.9% · 70% · 6 / 6: Art. 25 is in the gold label of 311 snippets. Summed over the 6 methods, it was predicted 1070 times (each method can predict it at most once per snippet, so the maximum is 6 × 887). 319 of those predictions were on a snippet whose gold label contains Art. 25 (correct) and 751 were not (hallucinations, so 319 + 751 = 1070). These 751 are 28.9% of the 2600 hallucinations made by all methods together, and 70% of the times a method predicts Art. 25 it is wrong (751 ÷ 1070). All 6 methods hallucinate it at least once. It is classed High because it has 100 or more hallucinations.
