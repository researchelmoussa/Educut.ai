Score per snippet: omission vs. hallucination, by method

For each snippet, every gold article the method missed is an omission (−1), every predicted article not in gold is a hallucination (+1), and every correctly predicted article counts 0. Score = hallucinations − omissions. Example: gold = {4, 5}, method = {7} → 2 omissions + 1 hallucination = −1. Bars: yellow below zero = omitted articles; blue above zero = correct articles (0 points, shown for context); orange stacked on top = hallucinated articles; black dot = net score. All 6 charts share the x axis: zoom or drag the slider at the bottom to move them together.
