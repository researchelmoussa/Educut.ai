NIAS-KNAW offers a fellowship for scholars and cultural actors at risk.

Safe Haven Fellowship
NIAS-KNAW | Netherlands
Field: Scholarship, literature, journalism and the arts

The fellowship lasts five months and pays a stipend of €3,500 per month. Scholarly proposals require a PhD and at least three years of postdoctoral experience. Literary, journalistic or artistic proposals require a bachelor's degree and at least seven years of professional experience. Applications are accepted only through the online form.

📅 Deadline (first semester 2027-2028): 14 December 2026, 12:00 CET
