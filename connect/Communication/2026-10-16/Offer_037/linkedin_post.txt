How do we build robots whose AI can be trusted outside the lab? A four-year doctoral programme in Edinburgh focuses on that question.

The programme starts in September 2027 and is full-time and in person only. All reference letters must arrive by the deadline.

First-round deadline: 27 November 2026. Later rounds are not confirmed, so please check the official apply page.
