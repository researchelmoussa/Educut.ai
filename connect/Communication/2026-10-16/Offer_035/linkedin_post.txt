A postdoctoral fellowship open to every discipline appears to be accepting registrations for its 2027 intake.

The Branco Weiss Fellowship supports early-career postdoctoral researchers in all disciplines, with funding of up to CHF 600,000 for up to five years. Interviews take place in Zurich, Switzerland, in June 2027.

Eligibility: your PhD defence must be passed before 15 January 2027, and your PhD obtained no more than five years before that date. Current or former faculty members are not eligible.

Registration deadline, as listed on the official timelines page: 15 January 2027, 5 p.m. CET. Please confirm the details on the official site before applying.
