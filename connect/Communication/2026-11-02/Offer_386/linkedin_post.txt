Planning your undergraduate studies abroad? National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan has published its application guidelines for international bachelor applicants. 🎓

Tsing Hua College International Bachelor Program (Fall 2027)
National Tsing Hua University, Tsing Hua College | Taiwan
Field: Undergraduate study (international bachelor program)

Requirements include TOCFL A2 or HSK level 4 for the Chinese-taught Group A, and, for English-taught tracks, for example IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 82. Applications are online only, one major per application.

Deadline: April 22, 2027, 12:00 noon Taiwan time.

Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
