Planning a Master's programme in the Netherlands starting in September? Leiden University offers a scholarship worth knowing about. 🎓

Leiden University Excellence Scholarship (LExS)
Leiden University | Leiden, Netherlands
Field: Master's programmes (all fields, with stated exceptions)

Who it is for: Master's students from non-EEA/non-EFTA countries starting in September. February starters are not eligible.

What you need: a motivation letter as a PDF of no more than 500 words.

Deadline: 1 December 2026. Late scholarship applications are not considered.

Please verify all details on the official Leiden University portal before applying.
