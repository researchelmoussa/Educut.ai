Considering a master's degree in the Netherlands? Radboud University has announced its scholarship call for the 2027/2028 intake.

Radboud Scholarship Programme | Radboud University
Nijmegen, Netherlands | Master's programmes

The programme offers up to 26 partial scholarships for students from non-EU/non-EEA countries entering a Radboud master's programme. Submit your application in OSIRIS by 31 January 2027 and complete it by 14 February 2027 (23:59 CET).

These details were found through search results, so please verify eligibility and dates on the official Radboud portal.
