Data science researchers: the University of Toronto's Data Sciences Institute has listed dates for its 2027 postdoctoral fellowship competition.

Data Sciences Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship
Data Sciences Institute, University of Toronto | Toronto, Canada
Field: Data science (interdisciplinary)

Candidates need a supervisor with a tenure or tenure-stream appointment at U of T, or an eligible PI at a funding partner institution. Candidate proposals are due 22 January 2027 (23:59 ET); supervisor forms and reference letters are due 29 January 2027.

The official page also shows an older banner, so please recheck the 2027 dates on the live page before applying.
