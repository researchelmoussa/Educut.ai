Neukom Postdoctoral Fellows
The Neukom Institute for Computational Science, Dartmouth College | Hanover, United States

These are two-year fellowships, with a third year on request, for computational research in any discipline, starting 1 September 2027. Applicants need a PhD in any discipline, or must expect to hold one by 1 September 2027. Letters of recommendation are due by 8 December 2026.

Deadline: 1 December 2026.
