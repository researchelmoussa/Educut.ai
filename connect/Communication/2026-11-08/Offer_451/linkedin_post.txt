Early-career researcher in climate, Earth system or sustainability science? Campus France has announced the MOPGA 2027 call.

MOPGA 2027: Visiting Fellowship Program for Early Career Researchers | Campus France (Make Our Planet Great Again programme)
France (host laboratories across the country) | Climate, Earth system and sustainability research

The programme offers 12-month research stays in French laboratories. Applicants must hold a PhD defended between December 2021 and December 2026, be of non-French nationality and not be living and working in France when applying. Applications close 15 December 2026.

Please verify eligibility and the thematic scope on the official Campus France portal.
