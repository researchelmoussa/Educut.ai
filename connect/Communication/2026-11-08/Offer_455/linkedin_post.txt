Interested in how human, animal and environmental health connect? The University of Helsinki has published dates for its international One Health master's programme.

One Health Master's Programme
University of Helsinki | Helsinki, Finland
Field: One Health (human, animal and environmental health)

The application period for studies starting in autumn 2027 runs from 5 January to 19 January 2027 (15:00 Helsinki time). Non-EU/EEA applicants pay a one-time national application fee of 100 euros.

Entry requirements are not listed here, so please verify them and the dates on the official portal before applying.
