Planning graduate studies in mathematics? The Berlin Mathematical School has announced its 2027 admission rounds.

Berlin Mathematical School (BMS) PhD Program, 2027 admission | Berlin Mathematical School (BMS)
Berlin, Germany | Mathematics

Phase I (master's studies) is for applicants completing a bachelor's degree by summer 2027; Phase II (doctoral research) requires a completed master's. The first round closes 1 December 2026, which is also the deadline for Phase I admission with scholarship (10 places). A second round runs until 1 April 2027.

Please verify the requirements and dates on the official BMS portal.
