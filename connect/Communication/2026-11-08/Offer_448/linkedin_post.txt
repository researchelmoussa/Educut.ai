Considering a master's degree in Copenhagen from outside Europe? The University of Copenhagen awards Danish Government Scholarships on academic merit.

Danish Government Scholarships
University of Copenhagen | Copenhagen, Denmark

They are for citizens of countries outside the EU/EEA/Switzerland who are admitted to a UCPH master's programme and hold a time-limited residence permit for study. There is no separate scholarship application: admitted applicants are considered automatically, and the number of awards is limited.

Master's admission deadline for September 2027: 15 January 2027, 23:59 CET.
