Considering a doctorate in economics in Latin America?

El Colegio de México, Centro de Estudios Económicos, in Mexico City, Mexico, is accepting applications to its Doctorado en Economía (Doctorate in Economics) for the 2027 intake.

Applications are submitted online through the Colmex admissions portal, from 1 October 2026 until 12 February 2027.

Entry requirements and funding are not covered in this summary, so read the official call and verify every detail on the Colmex portal before applying.
