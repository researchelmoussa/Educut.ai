Considering a career in public policy? The Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy at NUS accepts Master in Public Policy applications in an annual window.

Master in Public Policy (MPP)
Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, National University of Singapore | Singapore
Field: Public policy

The window runs from 1 August to 15 December each year, so this cycle closes on 15 December 2026 (11:59pm Singapore time). The application fee is waived for the MPP, and applicants who wish to be considered for a school scholarship must say so in their application.

Please verify intake details on the official LKYSPP portal.
