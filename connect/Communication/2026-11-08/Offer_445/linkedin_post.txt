Researching American art from outside the Washington, D.C. area? The Smithsonian American Art Museum offers a short-term research residency.

The Audrey Flack Short-Term Fellowship
Smithsonian American Art Museum (SAAM) | Washington, D.C., United States

It is open to predoctoral, postdoctoral and senior scholars of American art who live, work or study outside commuting distance of Washington, D.C. Applicants explain why a short-term residency suits them, for example because of financial, employment or caregiving constraints. Applications open 1 December 2026.

Deadline: 1 February 2027.
