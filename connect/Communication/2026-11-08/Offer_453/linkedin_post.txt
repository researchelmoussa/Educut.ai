Interested in a doctorate in the social sciences, humanities or cognitive science? Central European University in Vienna has published its 2027-2028 doctoral admissions.

Doctoral Programs at CEU (2027-2028 admission) | Central European University (CEU)
Vienna, Austria | Social sciences, humanities, law, network and cognitive science

Programmes include Political Science, Philosophy, Sociology and Social Anthropology, Comparative History, Network Science and Cognitive Science. Most require a master's degree (Cognitive Science is the exception). The deadline is 15 January 2027, 23:59 CET; applicants by then are automatically considered for the CEU Doctoral Scholarship.

Please verify programme details on the official CEU portal.
