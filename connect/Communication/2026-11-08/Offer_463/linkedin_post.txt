Planning a master's in medicine or life sciences in Sweden?

The KI Global Master's Scholarship at Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm, Sweden, is for tuition-fee-paying students applying to KI master's programmes starting autumn 2027.

To be considered, you must apply through University Admissions by 15 January 2027 with a KI programme ranked first. Only admitted students are considered in the final selection.

The separate scholarship application runs from 8 to 24 February 2027, with results on 26 April 2027. Please verify all details on the official KI portal before applying.
