Moving between plant biology and another research field? The Simons Foundation offers postdoctoral fellowships built around that shift.

Simons Postdoctoral Fellowships in Plant Biology
Simons Foundation | United States (hosted at U.S. nonprofit research institutions)

These three-year fellowships have two tracks: plant biologists training with mentors in other fields, or researchers from other fields applying their expertise to plant biology. Applicants need a PhD earned no earlier than three years before the deadline, or expected by the start date. U.S. citizens and foreign applicants may apply.

Deadline: 3 December 2026, 12 p.m. ET.
