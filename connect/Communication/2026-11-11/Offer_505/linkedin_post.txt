Translational medicine researchers: a new postdoctoral cohort is being formed in Copenhagen.

The BRIDGE – Translational Excellence Programme at the University of Copenhagen, Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences, in Copenhagen, Denmark, seeks 14 postdoctoral fellows in translational medicine for two-year appointments starting 1 September 2027.

Applicants need a PhD awarded less than 8 years before the deadline (PhD students defending before 1 September 2027 may apply) and must apply with a mentor team pairing a basic biomedical scientist with a clinical or industry scientist. Applications close 9 February 2027, 23:59 CET.
