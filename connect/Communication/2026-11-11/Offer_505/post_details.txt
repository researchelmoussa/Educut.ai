About: The University of Copenhagen seeks 14 postdoctoral fellows in translational medicine for two-year appointments starting 1 September 2027. - PhD awarded less than 8 years before the application deadline (leave periods can extend this)
- PhD students may apply if the thesis will be defended before 1 September 2027
- Not eligible: more than two years of postdoctoral employment at the University of Copenhagen before the fellowship starts
- Apply together with a mentor team (basic biomedical scientist plus clinical or industry scientist)
