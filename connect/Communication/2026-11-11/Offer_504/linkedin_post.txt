Committed to youth participation in democracy? This fellowship is worth a look.

The Hurford Youth Fellows Program is offered by the National Endowment for Democracy (NED) in Washington, D.C., United States. It is a four-month fellowship (1 May to 31 August 2027) focused on democracy and youth participation.

Applicants must be aged 30 or younger, with a demonstrated commitment to youth participation in democracy. Applications are due 30 November 2026, and recommendation letters by 4 December 2026.
