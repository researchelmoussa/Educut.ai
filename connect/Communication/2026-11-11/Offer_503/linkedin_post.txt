Researchers working on Slavic languages: a new call for papers is out.

Slavic NLP 2027 is run by the Slavic NLP 2027 workshop organisers (EACL 2027) in Athens, Greece. The field is natural language processing for Slavic languages, and the call is open to researchers and students.

Long papers use the two-column ACL format (up to eight pages plus two pages of references), and papers reviewed by ACL Rolling Review can be committed. Direct paper submissions are due 9 January 2027.

These details were found via search, so please verify them on the official workshop site.
