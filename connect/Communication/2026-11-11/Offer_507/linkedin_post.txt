Planning a master's or doctorate in South Korea? Here is a scholarship route to check.

Scholarships for International Students are offered by the Graduate School of Yeungnam University in South Korea, across all graduate fields. They are for new international master's and doctoral students recommended by their department, with award tiers based on TOPIK, IELTS, TOEFL iBT or TOEIC scores.

The scholarship is tied to admission: the Spring 2027 second-round online application closes 11 December 2026, 17:00 Seoul time.

Found via search: please verify scholarship terms and dates on the official Yeungnam portal.
