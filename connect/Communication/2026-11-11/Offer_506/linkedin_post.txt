Early-career sustainability researchers: here is a postdoctoral call worth knowing about.

The Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability Dean’s Postdoctoral Fellowship is offered by the Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability, Stanford University, in Stanford, United States. It runs for up to two years from September 2027 across Earth, oceans, energy, civil and environmental engineering and emerging sustainability fields.

Applicants need a PhD conferred within the last three years and at most two years of prior postdoctoral experience. Applications are due 30 November 2026.
