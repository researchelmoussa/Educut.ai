Architects and engineers considering a PhD: ETH Zurich has a call for doctoral fellows.

Doctoral Fellowships in Architecture and Technology are offered by ETH Zurich, Institute of Technology in Architecture (ITA) in Zurich, Switzerland.

Applicants need a university-level master's degree in architecture, engineering or a related field. The application package includes a cover letter naming one preferred professorship, a CV, a portfolio and a research statement. The submission deadline is 31 January 2027.

These details were found via search, so please verify them on the official ETH Zurich job portal before applying.
