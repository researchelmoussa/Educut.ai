Early-career researchers based in Spain or Portugal: this doctoral call is worth a careful read.

"la Caixa" Foundation has published the Doctoral INPhINIT Fellowships Retaining 2027, for doctoral research in any discipline in Spain or Portugal.

The call is open to any nationality. Applicants must have resided or carried out their main activity in Spain/Portugal for more than twelve months in the three years before the deadline, and be within the first four years (full-time equivalent) of their research career.

The call closes on 2 March 2027 at 14:00 (mainland Spain time). Please verify all details on the official portal.
