Postdoctoral researchers in health and biomedical sciences: a fellowship competition is under way in Toronto.

The Research Excellence Postdoctoral Fellows Program is run by the University of Toronto, Temerty Faculty of Medicine in Toronto, Canada, in health and biomedical research.

Candidates are nominated by a lab, and only one nominee per lab is selected from the 2027 competition. The application deadline is 31 January 2027; some U of T divisions may set earlier internal deadlines.

These details were found via search, so please verify them on the official Temerty Medicine page before applying.
