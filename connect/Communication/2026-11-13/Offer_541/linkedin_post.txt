Graduate students in mathematics: here is a research school that connects PDE theory with data science.

CIMPA (Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées) with Addis Ababa University is running "Modern Perspectives on Partial Differential Equations: Theory and Applications" in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, from 26 July to 6 August 2027. It covers PDE theory and numerics, with links to machine learning and epidemiology, and is designed for graduate students from East African universities.

Applications close on 26 March 2027. Please verify eligibility on the official CIMPA page.
