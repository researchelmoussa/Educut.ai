Deadline: 1 December 2026, 23:59 (Asia/Hong_Kong)
Compensation: Up to around HK$445,800 (US$57,150) in the first year and up to around HK$428,300 in each subsequent year
About: The University of Hong Kong's Presidential PhD Scholar Programme funds selected full-time PhD students admitted for 2027/28. All full-time PhD applicants are considered automatically; the Main Round closes 1 December 2026 (HKT), with later clearing rounds only if places remain.
Requirements:
- All applicants to the full-time PhD programme are considered for the scholarship; no separate application
- Main Round applicants are strongly encouraged to also apply for the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS); for 2027/28 all HKPFS holders receive the HKU Presidential PhD Scholarship
- Clearing rounds (to 30 April 2027 and 31 August 2027) consider applications only if places remain
