One PhD scholarship programme, open to applicants in every discipline, with no separate application needed.

The HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme at The University of Hong Kong (Hong Kong, Hong Kong) supports selected full-time PhD students admitted for 2027/28. Field: all disciplines.

Key points:
- All full-time PhD applicants are considered automatically, with no separate scholarship application.
- Main Round applicants are strongly encouraged to also apply for the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS).

Main Round deadline: 1 December 2026, 23:59 HKT (GMT+8). Please verify all details on the official HKU portal before applying.
