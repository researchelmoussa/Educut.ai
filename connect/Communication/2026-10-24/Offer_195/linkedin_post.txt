Planning a PhD? The University of Auckland offers doctoral scholarship rounds four times a year, open across disciplines. 🎓

The University of Auckland Doctoral Scholarship supports doctoral study at the University of Auckland in Auckland, New Zealand. Field: all disciplines.

Essential: endorsement from your proposed supervisor, so contact them well before the closing date. International applicants without a previous NZ student visa are advised to apply in the 1 November or 1 February rounds.

Next closing date: 1 November 2026. Some faculties list different dates, so please verify all details on the official University of Auckland portal.
