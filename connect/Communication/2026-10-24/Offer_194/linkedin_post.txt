Planning a PhD in Hong Kong for 2027-28? This CUHK scholarship scheme is worth knowing about. 🎓

The CUHK Vice-Chancellor's PhD Scholarship Scheme at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), Hong Kong, supports full-time PhD entrants across all disciplines.

There is no separate application: candidates who apply to a full-time PhD programme through the Online Application System by the deadline are considered automatically.

Main-round deadline: 1 December 2026, 11:59 pm Hong Kong time. Please verify all details on the official CUHK portal before applying.
