Thinking about a PhD in New Zealand? 🎓

Victoria University of Wellington offers the Wellington Doctoral Scholarship in Wellington, New Zealand, for doctoral study in all disciplines. The scholarship includes a stipend plus tuition fees.

Applicants normally hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree with academic distinction equivalent to First Class Honours, and must be eligible to enrol in a Doctoral degree at the university.

The current round closes 1 November 2026 at 4.30 pm (New Zealand time). Late or incomplete applications are not accepted. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
