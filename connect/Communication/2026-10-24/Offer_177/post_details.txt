About: Victoria University of Wellington offers Wellington Doctoral Scholarships for doctoral study, with three rounds a year. The current round closes 1 November 2026 at 4.30 pm; offers are made about six weeks after each closing date.
Requirements:
- Normally a Bachelor's or Master's degree with academic distinction equivalent to a First Class Honours degree at a New Zealand university
- Must be eligible, or reasonably expect to become eligible within three months, to enrol in a Doctoral degree at the university
- Online application and all supporting documentation, including references, must be received by 4.30 pm on the closing date; late or incomplete applications are not accepted
