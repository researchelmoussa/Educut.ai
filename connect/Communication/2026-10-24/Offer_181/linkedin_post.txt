Researchers working on neural networks and machine learning: the ESANN call for papers is open.

Organised by ESANN (European Symposium on Artificial Neural Networks), ESANN 2027 (35th European Symposium on Artificial Neural Networks, Computational Intelligence and Machine Learning) will be held 21-23 April 2027 in Bruges, Belgium, in hybrid mode.

The focus: artificial neural networks, computational intelligence and machine learning. Submissions are due before 18 November 2026, and a preliminary filter is applied before review. Accepted papers need at least one registered author.

Please verify all dates and submission details on the official ESANN portal before you submit.
