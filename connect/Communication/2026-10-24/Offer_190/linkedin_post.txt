Early-career AI researchers: KAUST is inviting new voices to present their work.

The KAUST Rising Stars in AI Symposium 2027 is calling for speakers. Hosted by King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in Thuwal, Saudi Arabia, the symposium in Artificial intelligence takes place in person on 1-3 February 2027.

The call is for PhD students, postdocs and early-career faculty who have recently made substantial contributions to AI. Speakers are selected on research merit, and travel and accommodation are covered for selected speakers.

Speaker applications close on 15 November 2026. Please verify all details on the official KAUST portal before applying.
