Graduate study in computer science and computational mathematics in Brazil.

The Instituto de Ciências Matemáticas e de Computação (ICMC), Universidade de São Paulo in São Carlos, Brazil, is selecting master's and doctoral students for 2027:
Mestrado e Doutorado em Ciências de Computação e Matemática Computacional (PPG-CCMC)

Applicants need a degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics or a related area by enrolment. Selection uses the academic transcript and Lattes CV; the doctorate also requires a research proposal.

Deadline: 5 November 2026 for the master's and the first doctoral flow. Please verify all details on the official portal.
