Student teams at U.S. schools: NASA is inviting ideas for a Moon Base.

The Moon Base Challenge from NASA invites teams from U.S. high schools and post-secondary schools in the United States to propose Moon Base solutions in power, construction, life support, surface mobility, communications and astronaut safety.

Eligibility: U.S. student teams only.

Registration through NASA STEM Gateway is open until 25 January 2027.

The status is likely open but not independently confirmed, so please verify eligibility, rules and dates on the official NASA Moon Base page.
