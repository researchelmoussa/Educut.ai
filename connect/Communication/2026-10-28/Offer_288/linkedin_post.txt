Undergraduate in Asia with a passion for biological research? 🔬

The National University of Singapore, Department of Biological Sciences, hosts the Amgen Scholars Program, a summer research internship in Singapore.

Field: Biological sciences and biotechnology research
Level: Undergraduate
Eligibility: the Asia Program is limited to undergraduate students attending colleges and universities across the Asia region.
