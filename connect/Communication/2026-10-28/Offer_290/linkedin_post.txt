Planning a postgraduate degree abroad? COLFUTURO has announced its 2027 call for Colombian graduates. 🎓

Programa Crédito Beca 2027
COLFUTURO | Colombia
Field: Postgraduate study abroad (any field)

The programme offers credit of up to USD 50,000, and up to 65% can become a scholarship if the beneficiary graduates and returns to Colombia.

Applicants need Colombian nationality and an undergraduate degree, and must present their programme of interest and a written essay.

Deadline: 4 March 2027. Please verify all dates and requirements on the official COLFUTURO portal before applying.
