Working in geometry or dynamical systems? A CIMPA research school is coming to Algeria.

"Geometry and Dynamics" is organised by CIMPA (Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées) with the National Higher School of Mathematics, Algeria. It takes place at Sidi Abdellah, Algiers, Algeria, from 23 May to 3 June 2027.

Field: Mathematics (geometry, dynamical systems)

Who it is for: CIMPA schools are aimed mainly at students and teacher-researchers in developing countries. Applications go through the CIMPA application site.

Deadline: 23 January 2027
