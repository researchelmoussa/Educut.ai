Analysis and differential geometry meet in San Salvador in July 2027.

Modern Techniques in Analysis and Differential Geometry is a research school run by CIMPA (Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées) with Universidad de El Salvador, in San Salvador, El Salvador, from 11 to 23 July 2027.

The programme ranges from geometric analysis on surfaces to reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces on compact Lie groups. CIMPA schools are aimed mainly at students and teacher-researchers in developing countries, and applications are made via the CIMPA application site.

Deadline: 11 March 2027. Please verify dates, eligibility and the application portal on the official CIMPA page before applying.
