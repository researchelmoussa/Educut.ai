The World Academy of Sciences (TWAS) runs TWAS Research Professors in LDCs, an academic programme in the natural sciences.

Research Professors are appointed for one year to visit host institutions in Least Developed Countries for two to four weeks. Host research groups submit the requests, so the host country varies.

Who is eligible: TWAS Members, Young Affiliates and Alumni with a PhD in the natural sciences (no age limit).

Deadline: 23 November 2026. Please verify all details on the official TWAS portal before applying.
