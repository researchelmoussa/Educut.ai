How can mathematical models and machine learning help us understand disease dynamics? 🧬

CIMPA (Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées) with Tribhuvan University is running the school "Mathematical Modeling and Machine Learning for Biomedicine and Public Health" in Kathmandu, Nepal, from 31 May to 9 June 2027.

Field: mathematical biology, machine learning and public health. Lectures and computer labs cover stochastic modelling and machine learning with mechanistic models such as PINNs.

CIMPA schools are aimed mainly at students and teacher-researchers in developing countries. Applications go through the CIMPA application site.

Registration deadline: 31 January 2027. Please verify details on the official CIMPA page.
