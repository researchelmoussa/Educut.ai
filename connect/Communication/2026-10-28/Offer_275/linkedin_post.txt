🌍 A UN fellowship for journalists covering sustainable development.

The DPoA MTR Journalism Fellowship is offered by the United Nations Office of the High Representative for the Least Developed Countries, Landlocked Developing Countries and Small Island Developing States (UN-OHRLLS) in Doha, Qatar.

Field: journalism on sustainable development. Open to journalists from the 44 Least Developed Countries; fellows must produce at least three substantive journalism outputs.

Ten fellows will receive travel, a daily allowance and briefings to report on the Doha Programme of Action High-level Midterm Review, 25-27 March 2027.

Deadline: 19 November 2026. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
