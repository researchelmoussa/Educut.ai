Planning a master's degree in Denmark? The University of Copenhagen considers admitted non-EU/EEA applicants for the Danish Government Scholarship.

Danish Government Scholarship
University of Copenhagen | Copenhagen, Denmark
Field: Master's degree programmes

There is no separate scholarship application: citizens of countries outside the EU, EEA or Switzerland who are offered admission to a master's programme are considered automatically.

Programme pages list 15 January, 23:59 (CET), as the application deadline for fee-paying applicants starting in September 2027. Deadlines can differ by programme, so please confirm yours on the programme page.

These details were found through search results, so please verify them on the official University of Copenhagen site.
