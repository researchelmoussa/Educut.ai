The Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) lists a Spring 2027 round for its Research Internship in Japan, in science and technology research.

Internships last 4 to 6 months between 1 April and 30 September 2027. The round is aimed at later-year bachelor's and master's students.

Applications require recommendation letters from professorial-level faculty, due the same day as the application. Deadline: 15 October 2026, 23:59 JST.
