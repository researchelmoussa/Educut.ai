How do cities shape the way people live and work? A fellowship for researchers exploring that.

The Mansueto Institute for Urban Innovation, University of Chicago (Chicago, United States) lists its Postdoctoral Fellowship in urban science for the 2027-2028 year.

Fellows come from economics, political science, sociology, public policy, urban planning and data science. Applicants need a PhD from outside the University of Chicago by the start date, and each fellow is supervised by a UChicago faculty member.

Full-time for one year, renewable for a second, at $70,000 per year with benefits.

Deadline: 6 December 2026, 5:00 p.m. CST. Verify all details on the official portal.
