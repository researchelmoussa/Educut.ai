Interested in hands-on research in the life sciences? Institut Pasteur hosts a summer research program for European undergraduates.

Program: Amgen Scholars Program
Institution: Institut Pasteur
Location: Paris, France
Field: Life sciences

The program runs from 1 July to 3 September 2027. It is open to undergraduates at a university in a Bologna Process country, in the second year of a three-year bachelor's or the second/third year of a four-year integrated program. Participants must attend the full program, including the September 2027 symposium in Cambridge.

The application site opens on 1 November 2026, and the stated deadline is 1 February 2027. Please verify all details on the official Institut Pasteur portal before applying.
