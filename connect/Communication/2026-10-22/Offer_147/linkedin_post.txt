Curious what scientific research looks like before you graduate? The Biozentrum at the University of Basel offers a summer research internship for bachelor students, with a monthly stipend.

Programme: Biozentrum Research Summer
Institution: Biozentrum, University of Basel
Location: Basel, Switzerland
Field: Life sciences, computational sciences

It is open to bachelor students in Biology, Nanosciences, Chemistry, Physics, Computational Sciences and related sciences. No prior research experience is required. Eligibility is limited to Swiss or EU citizens and holders of a Swiss C-permit.

Applications for 2027 run from 4 January to 28 February 2027 (12:00 noon CET). Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
