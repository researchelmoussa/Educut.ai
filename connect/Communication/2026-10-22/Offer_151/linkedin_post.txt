Considering a PhD in biomedical research in Paris?

Institut Pasteur's call for the Pasteur-Paris University International doctoral program (PPU) covers biomedical and life sciences in Paris, France, with about 15 openings a year on three-year contracts.

Applicants need a Master's degree completed by the summer before enrolment (not required at the deadline). Host labs choose candidates first, then a selection committee reviews them. Applications close 16 November 2026 (Paris time).
