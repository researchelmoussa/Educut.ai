Engineering graduates interested in sustainability: applications for the 2027-2029 intake of a two-year Erasmus Mundus Joint Master are scheduled to close in February.

European Master in Sustainable Systems Engineering
EMSSE (Erasmus Mundus Joint Master)

Field: Sustainable systems engineering
Who can apply: holders of a bachelor's-level degree (180 ECTS) in a related engineering or technical field; final-year students expecting to graduate by 31 July of the application year may also apply.
Deadline: 15 February 2027, 23:59 Paris time. Interviews run 18 to 20 March 2027.
