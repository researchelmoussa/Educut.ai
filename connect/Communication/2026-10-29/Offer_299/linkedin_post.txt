Robotics Institute Summer Scholars (RISS)
Carnegie Mellon University, Robotics Institute | United States
Field: Robotics research | Internship

The program is open to current undergraduate students only, and applicants who do not meet all eligibility criteria are not considered. Program dates are generally June 1 to August 15, with about 35 scholars selected from several hundred U.S. and international applicants.

Application deadline: January 20, 2027, 3 PM EST. Please verify dates and details on the official application portal before applying.
