Graduates from Sub-Saharan Africa planning a master's or doctorate closer to home: this DAAD programme is worth a look. 🎓

The In-Country/In-Region Programme Sub-Saharan Africa from DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) supports master's and doctoral studies at host universities in your own country or region.

Current calls cover programmes in Burkina Faso, Ethiopia, Ghana, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania and Uganda. Applicants apply through the DAAD portal and separately to the host university for admission.

Application deadline: 12 November 2026. Individual host-institution calls can set their own deadlines, so please verify all details on the official portal.
