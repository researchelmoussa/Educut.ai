About: Six-month Fields Institute postdoctoral fellowships attached to its thematic programs, which for 2027-2028 include geometric analysis and mathematical physics, and complex and algebraic dynamics. Applications go through MathJobs, with a 15 December deadline; the stipend is $35,000 per term.
Requirements:
- Six-month fellowships for participation in one of the Institute's major thematic programs
- Applicants solicited 12-18 months before the program; applications via MathJobs
- 2027-2028 thematic programs include Geometric Analysis and Mathematical Physics (July-December 2027) and Complex and Algebraic Dynamics in One Dimension (January-June 2028)
- Program organizers review applications and recommend candidates to the Fields Director
