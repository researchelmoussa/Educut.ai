Considering a PhD in science or engineering in Singapore? 🔬

The A*STAR Graduate Scholarship from the Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR) Graduate Academy in Singapore, Singapore supports PhD studies of up to four years at a Singapore autonomous university.

Field: Science and engineering PhD research.
Key requirements: at least a Second-Class (Upper) Honours degree (obtained or on track), and admission to a PhD programme at an autonomous university in Singapore.

Application deadline: 1 February 2027. Please verify all details on the official A*STAR portal before applying.
