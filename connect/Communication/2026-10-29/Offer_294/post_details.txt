Level: Doctoral
Deadline: 1 February 2027 (Asia/Singapore)
Compensation: not stated (do not mention pay)
About: A*STAR scholarship funding PhD studies of up to four years at a Singapore autonomous university. The first application cycle opens 1 November and closes 1 February; interviews follow from mid-February to March, with outcomes from mid-April.
Requirements:
- Singapore citizens or permanent residents: at least a Second-Class (Upper) Honours degree or equivalent in a relevant discipline (obtained or on track)
- Non-SPR foreigners: completed or on track to obtain at least a Second-Class (Upper) Honours degree at a Singapore autonomous university
- Must meet the criteria for, and gain admission to, a PhD programme at an autonomous university in Singapore
- Non-SPR foreigners serve a three-year bond in a Singapore-based company; no bond for citizens and permanent residents
