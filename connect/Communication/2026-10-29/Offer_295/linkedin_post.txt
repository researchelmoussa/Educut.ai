Dunedin is a UNESCO City of Literature, and the University of Otago marks it with a doctoral scholarship for literary research. 📚

University of Otago City of Literature PhD Scholarship
University of Otago | Dunedin, New Zealand
Field: Literature (PhD research)

Open to graduates in any discipline planning PhD research on a topic relevant to literature, who are obtaining their first doctoral qualification. One candidate is supported each year, with a NZ$34,128 annual stipend plus a domestic tuition fee waiver.

Deadline: 31 January 2027 (New Zealand time). Please verify current details and eligibility on the official University of Otago portal before applying.
