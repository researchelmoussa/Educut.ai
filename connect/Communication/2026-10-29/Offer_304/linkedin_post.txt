ETH Zurich is welcoming early-career researchers to apply for its postdoctoral fellowship programme. 🔬

ETH Postdoctoral Fellowships
ETH Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland
Field: All research fields at ETH Zurich

This two-year fellowship is applied for jointly with an ETH host professor. Applicants need a doctorate obtained at most two years before the deadline, plus at least one peer-reviewed journal publication or a prize for the doctoral thesis. Mobility rules apply.

Next deadline: 1 March 2027, 17:00 Swiss time. Please verify the call and all details on the official ETH portal before applying.
