Title: ETH Postdoctoral Fellowships

Location: Zurich, Switzerland · Onsite
Type: Postdoc · Fellowship
Field: All research fields at ETH Zurich
Level: Postdoctoral
Deadline: 1 March 2027, 17:00 (Europe/Zurich)
Compensation: Salary (100%, ETH standards) plus CHF 12,000 per year for research expenses
About: ETH Zurich's two-year postdoctoral fellowship for early-career researchers, applied for jointly with an ETH host professor. It covers the fellow's salary at ETH standards plus CHF 12,000 per year for research expenses. Calls close 1 March and 1 September, 17:00 Swiss time.
Requirements:
- Doctorate by the submission deadline (or within six months of it), obtained at most two years before the deadline
- At least one peer-reviewed journal publication or a prize for the doctoral thesis
- Mobility rules: no more than twelve months in Switzerland, and no more than six months at ETH Zurich, EPFL, PSI, WSL, Empa, Eawag or the University of Zurich, in the three years before the deadline; ETH/UZH doctorates ineligible
- Joint application with an ETH host professor via the eResearch system; all parts, including reference letters, in English
Skills: not stated
