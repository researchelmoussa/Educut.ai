Information retrieval researchers: the 50th ACM SIGIR conference has published its call for full papers. 🔍

The 50th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Research and Development in Information Retrieval (SIGIR 2027) takes place in San Jose, United States, from 18 to 24 July 2027.

Field: Information retrieval. Key requirements:
• The full author list is fixed at abstract submission, with no authorship changes afterwards
• All authors need OpenReview accounts before the abstract deadline

Abstract deadline: 14 January 2027 (AoE). Full papers follow on 21 January 2027 (AoE).
