TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship Program
International Cooperation and Development Fund (TaiwanICDF), Taiwan
Field: degree programmes at partner universities in Taiwan, for undergraduate and graduate students.

Key points:
- The 2027 online application covers both the TaiwanICDF Scholarship and the Taiwan-Europe Scholarship.
- Documents are also submitted through the ROC (Taiwan) embassy or representative office in your country.

Application window: 1 December 2026 to 15 March 2027. Please verify all details on the official TaiwanICDF portal before applying.
