Considering a doctorate in biomedical research? 🔬

Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine, in Singapore, is accepting applications for its PhD by Research Programme for the August 2027 intake.

Field: Biomedical and medical research.
Applicants need a Bachelor's degree with at least Distinction or Second Upper Class Honours (or equivalent), or an MBBS, plus confirmation from a proposed supervisor for a project chosen from the PhD Project Proposals list.

Application deadline: 31 December 2026 (Singapore time). Please verify all details on the official NTU portal before applying.
