Researching Japan through the humanities or social sciences? The Japan Foundation has a fellowship call open for 2027–28.

Japanese Studies Fellowship Program
The Japan Foundation | Japan
Field: Japanese studies (humanities and social sciences)

Categories: long-term research (4–12 months), short-term research (21–89 days) and doctoral fellowships (4–12 months). Doctoral applicants must be Ph.D. candidates in the humanities or social sciences. Fellowships must begin between 20 June 2027 and 20 March 2028.

Deadline: 1 December 2026, 1:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time (US applicants: 11:59 p.m. EST via The Japan Foundation, New York). Routes differ by country, so please verify the details on the official portal.
