Postdoctoral researchers in Argentina: CONICET has published its 2026 call for internal postdoctoral fellowships.

Becas Internas Postdoctorales (Convocatoria 2026)
Consejo Nacional de Investigaciones Científicas y Técnicas (CONICET), Argentina
Field: All scientific and technological disciplines

Applicants must have submitted the final copies of their doctoral thesis, or defended it, at the time of application. The call offers 800 fellowships of 36 months, starting 1 August 2027.

Applications run 18 February to 5 March 2027, with closing dates staggered by the last digit of the national ID (DNI). Please verify all details on the official CONICET portal.
