About: CONICET's internal postdoctoral fellowships (Convocatoria 2026) offer 800 three-year fellowships in Argentina starting 1 August 2027. Applications run 18 February to 5 March 2027, with closing dates staggered by national ID number.
Requirements:
- Only applicants who have submitted the final copies of their doctoral thesis or defended it at the time of application
- Electronic submission 18 February - 5 March 2027, staggered by the last digit of the national ID (DNI); DNI ending in 0 closes 1 March
- 800 postdoctoral fellowships; 70% for projects in Priority Topics and priority regions/areas
- Fellowships start from 1 August 2027 and last 36 months
