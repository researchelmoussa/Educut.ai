Scientists in developing countries: ICTP is inviting applications for research visits to Trieste.

The Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) has opened its Associates Programme, a fellowship in physics, mathematics and related sciences, based in Trieste, Italy.

Regular Associates are aged 36 to 45 and Senior Associates 46 to 65. Both need a PhD and a main affiliation in a developing country.

Deadline: 31 January 2027, with visits from 2028. Please verify all details on the official ICTP portal before applying.
