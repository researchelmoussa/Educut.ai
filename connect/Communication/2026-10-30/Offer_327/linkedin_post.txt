Planning a PhD in the United Kingdom? 🎓

The University of Reading is offering International PhD studentships for an October 2027 start, covering all disciplines offered for PhD study at the University.

Key points:
• Applicants must be eligible for International fees
• Apply for a PhD place and enter "International Research Studentship Competition" in the scholarship section
• Deadline: 15 January 2027

Each School nominates one candidate, and a university-wide panel decides around the end of March 2027.

Please verify all details on the official University of Reading portal before applying.
