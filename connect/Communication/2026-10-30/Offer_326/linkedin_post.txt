Planning a degree in Taiwan? National Taiwan University has set out its admissions timeline for international students entering in September 2027. 🎓

Admissions for International Degree Students (2027 September Entry)
National Taiwan University (NTU), Taiwan
Field: All NTU bachelor's, master's and PhD programmes

Applications are online only, and you can apply to up to five departments or graduate institutes. An application fee is charged per department.

Round 2 runs from 1 December 2026 to 19 January 2027 (Taipei time). Please check every date and detail on the official NTU admissions portal before you apply.
