Thinking about graduate study in data science and engineering? 📊

Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU), College of Science and Engineering, in Qatar lists its Master of Science in Data Science and Engineering, a 33-credit programme taking about two years.

📅 Deadline for international applicants: 1 February 2027.
