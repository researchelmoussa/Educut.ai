Planning a Master's or PhD with long-term ties to New Zealand? This scholarship supports postgraduate study at home or overseas.

William Georgetti Scholarship
Universities New Zealand, New Zealand
Field: postgraduate study in any field of importance to New Zealand (Master's and doctoral).

Key requirement: at least five years' residence in New Zealand immediately before the year of selection.

Awards: up to NZ$20,000 a year for Master's and NZ$30,000 a year for doctoral study in New Zealand, and up to NZ$45,000 a year overseas.

Applications close 1 February 2027. Please verify current details on the official Universities New Zealand portal before applying.
