A summer in a research lab is one of the clearest ways to test whether a scientific career is right for you. 🔬

The Weizmann Institute of Science in Israel runs the Kupcinet-Getz International Summer Program, an onsite research internship in the natural and exact sciences for undergraduate students.

Participants are placed in host labs from 16 June to 12 August 2027. Applicants choose up to five research topics for lab matching and submit a CV, transcripts, recommendation letters and a motivation letter.

The online application opens on 20 December 2026, and the deadline is 20 January 2027. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
