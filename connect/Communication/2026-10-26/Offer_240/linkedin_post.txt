The Data Science Summer Institute (DSSI) at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL), Data Science Institute, is an onsite internship in the United States for undergraduate and graduate students.

Students work with mentors on data science projects in one of two 2027 cohorts. Graduate applicants need a bachelor's degree and enrolment in a graduate program by Fall 2027; undergraduate applicants should be continuing students.

Deadline: 15 January 2027. Applications appear to be open, so please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
