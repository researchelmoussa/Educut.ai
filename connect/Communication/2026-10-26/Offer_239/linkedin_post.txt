DESY Summer Student Programme 2027
Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron DESY, Germany
Field: Physics, related natural sciences and computing

It is aimed at students with at least three years of university study in physics, related natural sciences or computing, who stay enrolled at a university for the whole programme.

The 2027 timeline lists online applications from early December until 31 January 2027. The portal is not open yet, so please verify all dates and details on the official DESY portal before applying.
