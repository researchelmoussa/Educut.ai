Working on artificial intelligence research? AAAI-27 brings 47 workshops to Montréal in February 2027. 🎓

The AAAI-27 Workshop Program is organised by the Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI) in Montréal, Canada, on 22-23 February 2027. Field: Artificial intelligence. It is aimed at researchers and students.
