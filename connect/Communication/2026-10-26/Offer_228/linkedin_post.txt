Graduate students in conservation: this is a funded fellowship worth knowing about. 🌍

World Wildlife Fund (WWF), Russell E. Train Education for Nature Program offers the Russell E. Train Fellowships for Conservation Leaders.

Applicants should be pursuing a master's or doctoral degree in conservation or a related field and be nationals of the eligible countries in Africa, Asia, the Pacific and Latin America listed on the official page.

Fellows can apply for up to US$15,000 per year, for up to two years.

Deadline: 15 December 2026, 11:59 PM US Eastern. Please verify eligibility and all details on the official WWF page.
