Thinking about an interdisciplinary year after your degree? 🎓

Ashoka University's Young India Fellowship (Class of 2028) is a one-year fellowship in interdisciplinary liberal studies in India, aimed at recent graduates.

Final-year undergraduates completing their degree by summer 2027 are also eligible. Candidates apply only once per admissions cycle, in one round.

Round 1 priority deadline: 29 November 2026. Later round deadlines are still to be announced. One Ashoka page says applications open on 19 November 2026, so please verify dates and details on the official portal.
