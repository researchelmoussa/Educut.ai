Professionals considering a master's degree in Sweden may want to look closely at this scholarship.

The Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals, offered by the Swedish Institute (Svenska institutet), supports master's studies at Swedish universities (eligible programmes) in Sweden for 2027/28. The city depends on the university.

Step one: apply to an eligible master's programme via UniversityAdmissions.se by 15 January 2027. Applicants from certain listed countries need at least 3,000 hours of work experience.

The scholarship portal opens on 8 February 2027 and closes on 24 February 2027 at 14:59 CET. Late applications are not accepted. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
