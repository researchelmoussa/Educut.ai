International students: the CAS-ANSO Scholarship 2027 call for science and technology master's and PhD study is open.

University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), in Beijing, China, has published the CAS-ANSO Scholarship 2027 (Master's and PhD at UCAS).

Eligibility: non-Chinese citizens with a valid passport; master's applicants born after 1 January 1997, PhD applicants after 1 January 1992; the language proficiency set out in the call.
Deadline for applicants and referees: 28 February 2027 (Beijing time).
You cannot apply through both USTC and UCAS.

The application status is listed as likely open. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
