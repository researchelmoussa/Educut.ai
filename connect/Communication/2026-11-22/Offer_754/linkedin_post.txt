Planning a master's degree in a development-related field? The World Bank's 2027 graduate scholarship call has published its dates.

The World Bank runs the Joint Japan/World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program (JJ/WBGSP) 2027 for developing-country nationals applying to listed development-related master's programmes.

Application Window 1 opens on 18 January 2027 and closes on 26 February 2027. Window 2 runs from 29 March to 21 May 2027; apply in the window that lists your target programme.

Applications were not yet open when this was recorded. Please verify dates, eligibility and the listed programmes on the official portal before applying.
