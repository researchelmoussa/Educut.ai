Humanities graduates and supervisors in the Netherlands: NWO has announced its 2027 call for humanities PhD positions.

PhDs in the Humanities 2027
Dutch Research Council (NWO) | Netherlands (participating humanities faculties)

The supervisor applies, with a tenured or tenure-track post at a participating faculty. Only candidates pre-selected by the faculty dean can be submitted, and faculties set earlier internal deadlines. Candidates need a (research) master's before 1 September 2027 and no funded PhD.

NWO deadline: 16 February 2027, 14:00 CET.

Status likely open but not independently confirmed: please verify on the official NWO page before applying.
