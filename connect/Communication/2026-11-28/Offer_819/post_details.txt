About: The Daughters for Life Foundation's 2027/2028 scholarship supports women from MENA countries in master's programmes at partner universities, including KU Leuven, the University of Antwerp, Ghent University and the University of the Fraser Valley.
Requirements:
- Female student who is a citizen and current resident of a MENA country (dual citizens or residents of countries outside the MENA region are not eligible)
- Demonstrated financial and socio-economic need and commitment to return to the home country after studies
- Minimum GPA of 75% or equivalent, community service or leadership record, and English proficiency for the chosen university
- Offer of admission from a DFL academic partner; applicants must apply to both DFL and the partner university
