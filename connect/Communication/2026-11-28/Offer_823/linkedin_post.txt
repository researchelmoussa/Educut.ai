Law graduates interested in international law: the International Court of Justice is welcoming university nominations for 2027-2028.

Judicial Fellowship Programme
International Court of Justice (ICJ) | The Hague, Netherlands

Candidates must be nominated by their university, which submits the application; the Court does not accept applications from individuals. Candidates should be no more than 31 years of age at the start of the programme, with possible exceptions.

Deadline: 5 February 2027 for university submissions. Internal university deadlines are likely earlier, so contact your law faculty soon.
