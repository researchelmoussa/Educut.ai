Planning a master's or PhD abroad? The "la Caixa" Foundation has announced its 2027 call.

Postgraduate Fellowships Abroad, from the "la Caixa" Foundation, covers postgraduate studies (master's or doctoral) in all disciplines except MBA. The call is for Spanish and Portuguese students starting full-time, attendance-based master's or doctoral studies with the fellowship. Applications are written entirely in English, with at least three reference letters.

Deadline: 21 January 2027, 14:00 (mainland Spain time).

Status is not yet independently confirmed, so please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
