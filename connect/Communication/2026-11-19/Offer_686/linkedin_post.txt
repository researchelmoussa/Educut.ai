Planning a master's in geo-information science or spatial systems? Note an early date.

The ITC Faculty Early Bird Programme is run by the University of Twente, Faculty of Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation (ITC), in Enschede, Netherlands. It is for students with unconditional admission to the MSc Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation or the MSc Spatial Systems & Society, enrolling at the University of Twente for the first time, starting September 2027.

Deadline: 31 January 2027.

The application form may not be open yet. Please verify all details on the official portal.
