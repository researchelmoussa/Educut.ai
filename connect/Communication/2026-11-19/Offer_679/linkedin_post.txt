Finishing a social sciences PhD? Grand Union DTP has announced its ESRC Postdoctoral Fellowships for early-career researchers.

Postdoctoral Fellowships
Grand Union DTP | United Kingdom

Field: Social sciences (ESRC). Fellows are based at Brunel, The Open University or Oxford, starting 1 October 2027. Candidates need a PhD awarded, or a viva passed with minor corrections, by the deadline, and no more than 12 months of active postdoctoral experience. Applications go through host departments, not directly to the DTP.

Expression-of-interest deadline: 11 January 2027 at 16:00 (UK time). Some departments set earlier internal dates.
