Early Career Fellowships
The Leverhulme Trust | United Kingdom
Field: All research disciplines
Tenable for 36 months at a UK host university.

Key eligibility: your doctoral thesis must have been submitted for viva voce examination not more than four years before the closing date. Applications are submitted through the host university.

The 2027 round opens on 1 January 2027. Closing date: 18 February 2027, 4pm UK time. Host universities may set earlier internal deadlines, so please verify all details on the official Leverhulme portal.
