Thinking about a PhD in Taiwan? Academia Sinica has announced its next international doctoral intake.

The Taiwan International Graduate Program at Academia Sinica in Taipei, Taiwan, invites PhD applicants for fall 2027 entry across multiple research programs.

Good to know: applicants can apply to only one TIGP program, and no application fee is required. Online applications open on November 1, 2026, and the deadline is February 1, 2027, 23:59 (GMT+8).

We found these details through search results for the official page, so please verify them on the official TIGP application portal before applying.
