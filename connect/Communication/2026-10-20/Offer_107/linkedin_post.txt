Considering a PhD in artificial intelligence in Germany? The Max Planck Artificial Intelligence Network has opened the call for its second cohort.

The MP-AIX Ph.D. Program, run by the Max Planck Artificial Intelligence Network (MP-AIX) in Germany, starts in 2027. Applications go through a joint ELLIS and MP-AIX portal, and one application can be considered for either program or both.

Two points to note: candidates already employed in a prospective advisor's group cannot receive MP-AIX funding, and reference requests must be sent through the portal before the deadline.

Deadline: 31 October 2026, 23:59 CET. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
