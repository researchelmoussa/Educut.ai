Planning a PhD in Hong Kong? The 2027/28 round of a major doctoral fellowship is open. 🎓

The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2027/28 is run by the Research Grants Council (RGC), Hong Kong, for PhD study at participating universities in Hong Kong, Hong Kong. It is open to applicants in all research disciplines, and this round offers 400 fellowships.

How it works: file an initial application with the RGC, then submit a full application to each chosen participating university before its deadline.

Initial RGC applications are due 1 December 2026. University deadlines may differ; verify all details on the official RGC portal.
