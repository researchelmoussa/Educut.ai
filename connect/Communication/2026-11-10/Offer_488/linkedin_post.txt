Thinking about graduate study in public policy? A Tokyo policy school has opened its 2027-2028 intake.

The National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies (GRIPS) in Tokyo, Japan is accepting applications for its Master's Programs (MP1, MP2), Admission 2027-2028, from applicants residing outside Japan.

The Online Registration Form may be submitted only once per academic year and is due on 30 April 2027, 17:00 JST. A complete set of supporting documents goes by post, and incomplete or late applications are not considered.

Please verify programme details and dates on the official GRIPS portal before applying.
