Thinking about a research career in physics or mathematics before starting a PhD?

ICTP has announced its call for the Postgraduate Diploma Programme at The Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) in Trieste, Italy.

This one-year programme in physics and mathematics combines two semesters of classes with a research project and dissertation.

Applicants need a degree equivalent to an MSc, or an exceptionally good BSc, in physics, mathematics or a related field, plus fluency in English.

Deadline: 28 February 2027.

Details were confirmed through search results of the official page, so please verify dates and requirements on the official portal before applying.
