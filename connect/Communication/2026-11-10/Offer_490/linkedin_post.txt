Interested in rigorous methods for analysing public policy? CIDE has published its 2027 master's call.

The Centro de Investigación y Docencia Económicas (CIDE) in Mexico is accepting registrations for its Maestría en Métodos para el Análisis de Políticas Públicas (METPOL), a master's in methods for public policy analysis.

Registration runs from 4 September 2026 to 15 February 2027 for applicants sitting exams at an embassy, consulate or SECIHTI site, and to 24 February 2027 at CIDE sites. Applicants may apply to only one programme per call, with no extensions.

Please verify all details on the official CIDE portal before applying.
