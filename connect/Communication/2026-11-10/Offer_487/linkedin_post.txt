How do universities in Africa work as ecosystems? CODESRIA is inviting research proposals.

The Council for the Development of Social Science Research in Africa (CODESRIA) has opened a call for Research Grants on University Ecosystems, covering themes such as higher education, gender and academic freedom.

Applicants need an affiliation with a recognised African higher education or research institution, with an affiliation or endorsement letter. Proposals can be individual or from teams of 3 to 5. Diaspora scholars may apply with a colleague at an African-based institution. The deadline is 16 January 2027.

Please verify all details on the official CODESRIA portal.
