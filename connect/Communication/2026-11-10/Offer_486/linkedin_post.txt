Researching Southeast Asia? A graduate forum in Singapore is inviting early-career scholars.

The Asia Research Institute, National University of Singapore invites applications to the 22nd Singapore Graduate Forum on Southeast Asian Studies. It runs online on 7-11 June 2027 and at NUS in Singapore on 14-18 June 2027.

It is open to PhD students who have completed fieldwork and to recent PhD recipients (awarded 2025 or later). Applicants submit a 300-400 word abstract and a recommendation letter. Applications close on 4 December 2026.

Please verify all details on the official ARI page before applying.
