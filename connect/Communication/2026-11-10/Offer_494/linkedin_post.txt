Planning a master's degree in the biomedical sciences?

Mohammed Bin Rashid University of Medicine and Health Sciences (MBRU) is accepting applications for its Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

This is a two-year, full-time programme for the 2027-28 intake.

Applicants need a bachelor's degree in a biological sciences discipline with a minimum cGPA of 3.00/4.00, plus English proficiency (IELTS or TOEFL).

Deadline: July 12, 2027. Early submissions receive priority.
