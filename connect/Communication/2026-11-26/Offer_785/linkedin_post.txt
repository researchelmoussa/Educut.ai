Computational neuroscience in Okinawa: OIST has announced its 2027 course.

OIST Computational Neuroscience Course (OCNC 2027)
Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) | Okinawa, Japan

The course runs from 5 to 22 July 2027 and is aimed at young researchers with theoretical or experimental backgrounds. Participants formulate a modelling project to work on during the course.

Applications open on 1 January 2027. Deadline: 31 January 2027. OIST notes the course may be cancelled if building renovations run over time.

The status is likely open but not independently confirmed, so please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
