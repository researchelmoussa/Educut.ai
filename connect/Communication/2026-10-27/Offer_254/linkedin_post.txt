Mathematics students planning their 2027 summer: IMPA has announced its Summer Program for 2027.

Programa de Verão 2027 at IMPA - Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada, Brazil, offers mathematics courses for undergraduate and graduate students from 4 January to 26 February 2027.

Course registration opens on 15 December 2026 and closes on 18 January 2027. Applicants register in the "IMPA Institucional" online system and provide a photo ID, recommendations from at least two professors and an official transcript.

Dates may still change, so please verify all details on the official IMPA portal before applying.
