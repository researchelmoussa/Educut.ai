Considering a doctorate in artificial intelligence and data science in Mexico? 🎓

Instituto Politécnico Nacional (IPN), Centro de Innovación y Desarrollo Tecnológico en Cómputo (CIDETEC) lists its "Doctorado en Ciencia y Tecnología de Inteligencia Artificial y Ciencia de Datos", a PhD programme offered at five IPN sites in Mexico.

It is aimed mainly at applicants with a master's degree in computing, engineering, exact sciences or related fields. Selection criteria in the previous call included a GRE or EXANI-III score, English proficiency and an interview.

Applicant registration closes on 16 April 2027. Please verify all details on the official IPN portal before applying.
