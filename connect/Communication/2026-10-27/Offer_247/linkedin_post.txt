Considering a master's in AI and data science in Mexico? 🎓

The Instituto Politécnico Nacional (IPN), Escuela Superior de Cómputo (ESCOM) lists an admission round for its "Maestría en Ciencia y Tecnología de Inteligencia Artificial y Ciencia de Datos", a Master's programme in Mexico in artificial intelligence and data science.

Applicants need a bachelor's degree and must pass the admission exam and the interview. Registration is listed to close on 16 April 2027.

These details were seen through search results of the official page, so please verify dates and requirements on the official ESCOM-IPN portal before applying.
