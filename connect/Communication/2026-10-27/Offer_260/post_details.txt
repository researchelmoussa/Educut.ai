About: The Royal Society's Career Development Fellowship supports early-career researchers of Black heritage with UK links, offering up to £703,800 over four years. Applicants need a PhD and under 24 months of postdoctoral experience. Applications close 18 November 2026; decisions by 30 June 2027.
Requirements:
- Self-identify as Black heritage, including mixed Black background
- UK nationality, or part of education completed in the UK, or a UK postdoctoral post held
- PhD, or PhD by the time funding starts
- Fewer than 24 months of postdoctoral experience (career breaks taken into account)
- Applicants needing a visa are eligible for the Global Talent Visa fast-track endorsement
