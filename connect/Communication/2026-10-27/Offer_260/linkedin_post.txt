Early-career researchers in science: The Royal Society runs a funded fellowship for researchers of Black heritage with UK links. 🔬

Career Development Fellowship
The Royal Society | United Kingdom (host institution depends on the applicant)
Field: Science (Royal Society remit)

Key requirements:
• A PhD, or a PhD by the time funding starts
• Fewer than 24 months of postdoctoral experience (career breaks taken into account)

Funding: up to £703,800 over four years.
Deadline: 18 November 2026

Please verify eligibility and dates on the official Royal Society portal before applying.
