About: STIAS's Iso Lomso Fellowship is a three-year award for early-career researchers from African countries affiliated with African institutions, with residencies at STIAS. The 2027 call opens 15 October 2026 and closes 15 February 2027; results are usually announced in June.
Requirements:
- National of an African country
- Affiliation with a research or higher-education institution in Africa, expected to be maintained
- Doctorate obtained within the preceding seven or eight years from 1 January of the call year (STIAS pages differ)
- Completed postdoctoral fellowship or equivalent post-PhD research programme
- Not more than 42 years of age on 1 January of the call year
- Able to begin a first residency within the first year of the fellowship
