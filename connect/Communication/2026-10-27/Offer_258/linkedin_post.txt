Early-career researcher connected to an African institution? This fellowship is worth a closer look. 🌍

The Stellenbosch Institute for Advanced Study (STIAS) in South Africa offers the Iso Lomso Fellowship, a three-year award for early-career researchers in any discipline, with residencies at STIAS.

Key eligibility points include being a national of an African country and holding an affiliation with a research or higher-education institution in Africa. Applicants should also have completed a postdoctoral fellowship or equivalent post-PhD research programme.

The 2027 call closes on 15 February 2027. Please verify all details on the official STIAS portal before applying.
