Antimalarial research and organic synthesis come together in a new postdoctoral opening in South Africa. 🔬

The University of the Witwatersrand (Wits), School of Chemistry, South Africa, offers a Postdoctoral Fellowship in Organic Synthesis (antimalarial hit compounds).

Candidates need a PhD in synthetic organic chemistry obtained within the past five years, plus practical experience in organic synthesis and analytical techniques.
