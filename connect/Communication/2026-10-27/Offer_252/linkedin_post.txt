Funding for an engineering research master's in Colombia. 🎓

Universidad de los Andes, Facultad de Ingeniería offers the Beca Avanza 2027-10 scholarship in Colombia for engineering research master's programmes.

It covers tuition for up to eight credits per semester, plus pay as an 8-hour-a-week research monitor, for up to four semesters. One award per programme.

Applicants must be admitted to an in-person research master's programme for 2027-10, or be an undergraduate degree candidate. A motivation letter and evidence of prior contact with a faculty member or research group director are required.

Applications close on 26 November 2026. Verify details on the official portal.
