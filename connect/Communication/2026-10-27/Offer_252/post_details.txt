Location: Colombia · Onsite
Type: Scholarship
Field: Engineering (research master's programmes)
Level: Master's
Deadline: 26 November 2026
Compensation: Tuition up to 8 credits per semester plus a research-monitor payment of COP 2,729,376 (8 hours per week)
About: Uniandes Engineering's Beca Avanza 2027-10 covers tuition for up to eight credits per semester in a research master's, plus pay as an 8-hour-a-week research monitor, for up to four semesters. One award per programme; applications close 26 November 2026, results 10 December.
Requirements:
- Admitted to an in-person research master's programme of the Faculty of Engineering for 2027-10, or an undergraduate degree candidate
- Not eligible if already holding another scholarship or tuition funding, or already active in an in-person engineering research master's at Uniandes
- Motivation letter, undergraduate GPA evidence and evidence of prior contact with a faculty member or research group director
- Renewal requires a cumulative average of at least 4.0
- One scholarship per programme (nine in total)
