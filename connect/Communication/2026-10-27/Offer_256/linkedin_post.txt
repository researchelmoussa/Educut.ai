Massey graduates planning a doctorate: this scholarship is worth a close look.

The Massey University Alumni Doctoral Scholarship from Massey University in New Zealand supports doctoral study in any field. 🎓

It offers $90,000 plus fees over three years. Applicants need a Massey University degree and an offer of place in a Massey doctoral programme, or enrolment within the first 12 months of a doctorate by the closing date.

Applications open on 1 November 2026 and close on 1 February 2027. Please verify all details on the official Massey portal before applying.
