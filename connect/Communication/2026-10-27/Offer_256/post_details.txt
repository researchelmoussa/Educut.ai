Deadline: 1 February 2027
Compensation: $90,000 plus fees, over three years
About: Massey University's Alumni Doctoral Scholarship gives Massey graduates starting or early in a Massey doctorate $90,000 plus fees over three years. International and domestic, full- and part-time candidates can apply. Applications open 1 November 2026 and close 1 February 2027.
Requirements:
- Hold a Massey University degree (undergraduate or postgraduate)
- Hold an offer of place in a Massey doctoral programme, or be enrolled within the first 12 months of a doctorate by the closing date
- International and domestic, full-time and part-time candidates are eligible
- Successful applicants must enrol by 1 June of the award year
