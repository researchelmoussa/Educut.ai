When conflict or a natural or biological hazard interrupts a research project, getting it back on track can be hard. 🌍

UNESCO and The World Academy of Sciences (TWAS) offer the Research Continuity Grants for Africa: six grants of up to USD 2,000 each in the natural sciences. This is a multi-country programme for researchers across Africa.

The grants are for African scientists based at recognized universities or research institutions in Africa whose natural-sciences research was interrupted by conflict or by natural or biological hazards.

Deadline: 15 November 2026. Please verify all details on the official TWAS/UNESCO portal before applying.
