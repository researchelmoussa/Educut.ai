Considering a master's degree in computer science? Finland is worth a closer look.

The University of Helsinki lists its Master's Programme in Computer Science for studies starting in autumn 2027.

📍 Finland
🎓 Field: Computer science
🗓️ Application period: 5 January 2027 (08:00) to 19 January 2027 (15:00, Helsinki time)

Essential requirement: a completed bachelor's degree or equivalent. Applicants graduating after the application period can apply, with a conditional offer. There is one intake per academic year, and the online form opens only during the application period.

Please verify all details on the official university portal before applying.
