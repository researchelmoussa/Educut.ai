Taiwan Australian National University Scholarship
The Australian National University (ANU), Australia
Field: Doctoral research (any ANU discipline)

Key requirements: citizenship of Taiwan, and admission to full-time PhD study at ANU starting between 1 May and 31 August 2027. A separate application to Taiwan's Department of International and Cross-Strait Education is also required.

Application deadline: 30 November 2026.

These details were confirmed through search results, so please verify everything on the official ANU portal before applying.
