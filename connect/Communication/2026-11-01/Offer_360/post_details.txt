- Citizens of Taiwan

- Study must begin between 1 May 2027 and 31 August 2027
- Applicants must also apply to Taiwan's Department of International and Cross-Strait Education (window 9 Nov 2026 to 25 Nov 2026, midday)
- Selection based on consistently high academic results
