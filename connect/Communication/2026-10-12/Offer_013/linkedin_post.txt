Considering a social sciences PhD in the United Kingdom? 📚

The SEDarc Studentship Competition 2027-2028 offers ESRC-funded doctoral studentships through the SEDarc partnership for 2027 entry. It is open to UK and international students, with UK home fees and a stipend.

Field: Social Sciences
Location: United Kingdom (city depends on the partner university)

Essential step: applicants must first be nominated by one of six partner universities, each with its own earlier internal deadline. The SEDarc deadline is 29 January 2027 at 16:00 GMT.
