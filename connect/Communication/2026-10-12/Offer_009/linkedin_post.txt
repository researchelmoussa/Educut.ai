Life sciences students: EPFL is offering eight weeks of hands-on research in summer 2027.

The EPFL School of Life Sciences Summer Research Program runs from July 5 to August 27, 2027, at the EPFL School of Life Sciences in Lausanne, Switzerland.

It is open to Bachelor and first-year Master students enrolled in a life sciences field, with a minimum GPA equivalent to 3.75/4.0 or a place in the top 5% of their class.

Deadline: 15 November 2026, 23:59 CET.
