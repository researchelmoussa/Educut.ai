A scholarship pathway to graduate study at the University of Cambridge, designed for candidates from Central Asia and neighbouring countries.

The University of Central Asia Cambridge Scholarship Programme (Academic Year 2027-2028) is offered by the University of Central Asia for study in Cambridge, United Kingdom. Field: Graduate study.

It is for candidates from Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, with applicants from Afghanistan, Pakistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan also considered. Shortlisted applicants then submit a full Cambridge application.

Deadline: 12 November 2026, 23:59 (GMT+6).
