Planning a postdoctoral research stay in Japan? 🔬

The Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) runs the Postdoctoral Fellowships for Research in Japan (Standard), open across multiple fields, with host institutions in Japan.

This is the FY2027 Standard open call, 2nd recruitment round. Essential requirement: a host researcher in Japan submits the application to JSPS through the head of their institution, so securing a host comes first.

JSPS deadline: 23 April 2027, 5 p.m. JST. Host institutions may set earlier internal deadlines.
