Planning graduate studies in Japan? 🎓

Hokkaido University offers the MEXT Scholarship: IGP Special Quota, a Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship quota for its International Graduate Program in Sapporo, Japan, for October 2027 admission.

Field: Life Sciences / Science
Level: Master's and PhD applicants

Essential first step: secure a supervisor's agreement. Applicants contact supervisors by 10 November 2026, and the supervisor submits documents to the IGP office by 10 December 2026. The official page labels these dates as tentative.
