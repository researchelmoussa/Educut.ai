Public policy shapes how societies respond to their biggest challenges, and an Erasmus Mundus joint master's is one route into the field.

The Joint Master of Arts in Public Policy (Mundus MAPP) is coordinated with Central European University. Field: public policy. It is aimed at Master's applicants.

Those who apply by the scholarship deadline are automatically considered for merit-based scholarships. Mundus MAPP applicants pay no application fee unless they also apply to other CEU programs.
