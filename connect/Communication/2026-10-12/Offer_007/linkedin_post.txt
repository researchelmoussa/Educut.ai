Bachelor and Master students in engineering: EPFL is taking applications for its summer 2027 research internships.

The E3 EPFL Excellence in Engineering Program brings students from other universities to EPFL in Lausanne, Switzerland, for a summer research internship in engineering.

You must stay enrolled at your home university for the whole internship. The application needs a CV, transcripts, a research statement and ID.

Deadline: 1 November 2026.
