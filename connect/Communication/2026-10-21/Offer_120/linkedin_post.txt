Planning a Master's at ETH Zurich? 🎓

ETH Zurich's Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP) supports Master's students starting in autumn 2027. About 60 scholarships are planned.

📍 Zurich, Switzerland
Funding: CHF 12'000 per semester, from HS27 onwards 13'500 CHF, plus a tuition fee waiver.

Applications are accepted only in the first application window, 1-30 November 2026, and include uploading a Master's thesis pre-proposal.
Deadline: 30 November 2026, 23:59 (Zurich time).

Please verify all details on the official ETH Zurich portal before applying.
