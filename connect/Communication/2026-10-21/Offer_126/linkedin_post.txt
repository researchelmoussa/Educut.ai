One postdoctoral appointment, two leading research institutions: one in Germany, one in Israel. 🔬

The Max Planck-Weizmann Postdoctoral Programme is now in its seventh application round. It is offered by the Max Planck Society and Weizmann Institute of Science.

It is a four-year fellowship split between the Max Planck Society in Germany and the Weizmann Institute in Israel, for positions starting between March and September 2027.

Eligible research fields differ between the official pages, so please verify eligibility and every detail on the official application portal before applying.

Application deadline: 21 December 2026.
