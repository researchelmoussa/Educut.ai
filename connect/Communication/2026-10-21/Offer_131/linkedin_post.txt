Planning a two-year MSc in the Netherlands? TU Delft offers a named excellence scholarship for international students. 🎓

Opportunity: Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships
Institution: Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)
Location: Delft, Netherlands
Level: Master's

The scholarships are for international applicants (conditionally) admitted to a 2-year regular TU Delft MSc programme. At least 16 are awarded each year. Current TU Delft bachelor students and holders of Dutch bachelor degrees are not eligible.

Deadline: the complete MSc and scholarship application is due 1 December 2026 (23:59 CET).

These details were confirmed through search results of the official page, so please verify them on the official TU Delft portal before applying.
