Mathematics researchers: IMPA in Rio de Janeiro has opened applications for its summer postdoctoral program. 📐

Summer 2027 Postdoctoral Program
IMPA - Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil | Mathematics | Postdoctoral

The program runs in January and February 2027 at IMPA's Rio de Janeiro campus and is intended for active researchers with a PhD-equivalent degree.

Registration deadline: November 3, 2026, according to IMPA's notice. Please verify all details on the official IMPA portal before applying.
