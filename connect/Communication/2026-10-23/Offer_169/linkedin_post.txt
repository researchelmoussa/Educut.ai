Planning a postdoc in cardiac or cancer research? The University of Cape Town has a 2027 call in health sciences. 🔬

Faculty of Health Sciences 2027 Postdoctoral Fellowship
University of Cape Town, Faculty of Health Sciences | Cape Town, South Africa
Field: Health sciences | Level: Postdoctoral

Fellowships are offered in three areas: cardiac research, cancer research and an open category.

Key points:
• You need a UCT host or supervisor
• The host/supervisor submits the documentation to the Faculty Research Office

Closing date: 6 November 2026 (South African time).

Please verify eligibility and all details on the official UCT fellowships noticeboard before applying.
