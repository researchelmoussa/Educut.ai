About: Imperial College London's President's PhD Scholarships fund new PhD students across disciplines, covering tuition fees and a stipend. There is no separate form: departments nominate admission applicants. Round 1 closes 2 November 2026; later rounds 11 January and 1 March 2027.
Requirements:
- First class or equivalent undergraduate/integrated Master's, or a distinction in a standalone Master's (departments may nominate special cases)
- Open to new PhD applicants only; current Imperial PhD students cannot apply
- No nationality restrictions; full- and part-time study
- An Imperial supervisor must agree to supervise you before applying
- Apply for admission through Imperial's online system and tick the scholarship option; two academic referees
