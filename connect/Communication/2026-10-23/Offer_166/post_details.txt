About: ANID's national doctoral scholarship funds PhD study in accredited programmes at Chilean universities, in all fields, for up to four years. Chilean and foreign applicants may apply; up to 1,000 scholarships. Applications and recommendation letters close 27 November 2026, 13:00 Chile time.
Requirements:
- Licenciatura, professional title or equivalent (including degrees from abroad)
- Admitted, formally applying, or enrolled in an accredited doctoral programme at a Chilean university by the close of applications
- Academic excellence: graduation average of 5.0/7.0 or above, or top 30% of the graduating ranking
- Open to Chilean and foreign nationals, with or without permanent residence
