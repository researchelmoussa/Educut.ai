Compensation: S/ 4,000 monthly allowance for 12 months (renewable up to two times); academic and enrolment fees covered
About: PUCP's doctoral scholarship for the 2027-1 intake gives admitted PhD students a monthly allowance of S/ 4,000 for 12 months, renewable up to two times, with academic and enrolment fees covered. The call runs 10 August to 4 November 2026.
Requirements:
- Master's degree and endorsement from a PUCP adviser linked to the doctoral programme
- English at B2 or higher
- Peruvian or foreign nationals studying with exclusive dedication; admission to the doctoral programme required
- PUCP full-time faculty and full-dedication staff excluded
- Documents: personal essay, research project, CV, two recommendation letters, adviser commitment letter, ethics declaration, English certificate
Skills: not stated
