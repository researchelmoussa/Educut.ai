Planning a PhD in Lima? PUCP has an open call for doctoral funding. 🎓

Beca Doctoral PUCP
Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP), Escuela de Posgrado, Lima, Peru
Field: PUCP doctoral programmes

Applicants need a master's degree with endorsement from a PUCP adviser linked to the doctoral programme, plus English at B2 or higher.

The scholarship offers a monthly allowance of S/ 4,000 for 12 months (renewable up to two times), with academic and enrolment fees covered.

Deadline: 4 November 2026 (Lima time). Please verify dates and eligibility on the official PUCP portal before applying.
