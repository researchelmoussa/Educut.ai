Researching where cybersecurity meets artificial intelligence? Tel Aviv University's Blavatnik Interdisciplinary Cyber Research Center has published its 2027-2028 call.

Call for Visiting Research Fellows
Blavatnik Interdisciplinary Cyber Research Center (ICRC), Tel Aviv University
Tel Aviv, Israel
Field: Cybersecurity, artificial intelligence

The fellowships last about 4 to 9 months (October 2027 to June 2028) and are open to scholars and PhD candidates in cybersecurity, AI and related fields. They are non-stipendiary: fellows arrange their own funding.

Deadline: 27 February 2027 (timezone not stated). Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
