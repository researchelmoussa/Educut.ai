About: Ghent University's Special Research Fund offers PhD "sandwich" scholarships for Global South candidates: 24 months funded within a 48-month PhD split between Ghent and a partner institution. About 15 scholarships yearly; earliest start 1 October 2027.
Requirements:
- Candidates must come from and hold the nationality of one of the selected Global South countries
- Proposal submitted jointly by the candidate, a Ghent University supervisor and a supervisor at the partner university
- Co-funding from the partner institution is mandatory
- Exception requests (funding or mobility rules) due by 15 January 2027
