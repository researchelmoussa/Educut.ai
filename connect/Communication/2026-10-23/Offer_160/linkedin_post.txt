Ghent University's Special Research Fund has a 2027 call for Global South doctoral researchers. 🎓

Doctoral Scholarships for Candidates from the Global South - call 2027
Ghent University | Ghent, Belgium
Field: All disciplines | Level: PhD

These "sandwich" scholarships fund 24 months within a 48-month PhD split between Ghent and a partner institution.

Key requirements:
• Nationality of one of the selected Global South countries
• A proposal submitted jointly with a Ghent University supervisor and a partner-university supervisor

Deadline for project proposals: 9 February 2027 (Belgian time).

Please verify all details on the official Ghent University page before applying.
