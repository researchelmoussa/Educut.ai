Science undergraduates at CityU partner universities: a summer research exchange in Hong Kong for 2027.

City University of Hong Kong (CityU), College of Science runs the Inbound Summer Exchange Programme in Hong Kong from 14 June to 6 August 2027. Participants register for the 6-credit course CSCI4006 Summer Research Project for Science Students.

Applicants must be full-time students at a CityU partner university for at least one academic year and be nominated by their home university. The application deadline is 15 February 2027. Please verify details and the application window on the official portal.
