Ten years or more into a foreign policy or national security career? This fellowship offers a year of strategic conversation.

The Strategy and Statecraft Fellowship from the Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) brings fellows together for monthly dinners in Washington, D.C., United States, on foreign policy, national security and strategic thought.

Who it is for: U.S. citizens or permanent residents with ten or more years of experience in their field, living in or able to travel to the Washington, D.C. area.

Deadline: 13 December 2026 (US Eastern Time). Please verify all details on the official CSIS portal.
