Planning a PhD in engineering or the physical sciences? UCL has published its doctoral projects for 2027/28.

The UCL EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Award (UELA) Studentships at University College London (UCL), in London, United Kingdom, cover engineering and physical sciences, with study starting 1 October 2027.

Applicants choose up to three of 133 advertised projects, and two references must be submitted by the application deadline. International places are capped at 30%.

Deadline: 8 January 2027 (UK time). UCL advises submitting applications and references by 16 December 2026. Please verify all details on the official UCL portal.
