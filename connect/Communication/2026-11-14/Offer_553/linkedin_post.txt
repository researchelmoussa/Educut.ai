Researchers from East and West Africa: CEGA has published its 2027-2028 fellowship call.

CEGA Fellowship Program: 2027-2028 Request for Applications
Center for Effective Global Action (CEGA), University of California, Berkeley, Berkeley, United States
Field: Development economics and global development research

Fellows spend 16 weeks in residence at UC Berkeley or virtually, in Fall 2027 or Spring 2028. Applicants must be from an eligible East or West African country and plan to return to an institution in the region. Applications close 17 January 2027, 5 PM GMT.
