Hanoi will host a summer school in probability and mathematical physics, held alongside ICMP 2027.

VIASM-IAMP Summer School 2027 on Probability and Mathematical Physics
Hosted by the Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics (VIASM) in Hanoi, Vietnam, from 23 to 28 July 2027. It is a satellite event of ICMP 2027 in Da Nang, with four mini-courses and ten contributed talks by young participants.

Registration is compulsory and in person, through an online form. Deadline: 1 July 2027. Please verify the details on the official portal.
