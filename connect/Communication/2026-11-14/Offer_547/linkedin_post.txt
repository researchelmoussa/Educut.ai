Machine learning meets many-body physics at a summer school in Colorado.

Boulder School 2027: Machine Learning and Condensed Matter is run by the Boulder School for Condensed Matter and Materials Physics, University of Colorado Boulder, in Boulder, United States.

The school runs 5-30 July 2027 and introduces graduate students to the exchange of ideas between machine learning and condensed matter physics. Organisers strongly discourage attending only part of the session.

Application deadline: 15 January 2027.
