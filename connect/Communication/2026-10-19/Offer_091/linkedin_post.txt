Early-career researchers linked to Switzerland: the SNSF has a February 2027 call for its mobility fellowship. 🔬

Postdoc.Mobility, from the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF), supports early postdoctoral researchers in all research disciplines during a research stay abroad.

Note: proposals can be submitted no earlier than 6 months before the thesis defence, and non-Swiss applicants without a Swiss settlement permit generally need a Swiss doctorate or state examination and no prior postdoc abroad. Submission is via mySNF.

Deadline: 2 February 2027, 17:00 CET.

Eligibility rules are being revised, so please verify every detail on the official SNSF portal.
