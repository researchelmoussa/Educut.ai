Thinking about spending next summer in a research lab in Germany? 🔬

DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) runs RISE Germany: three-month summer research internships at universities and institutes across Germany, starting between 15 May and 15 July 2027. The host city depends on the internship you choose.

Field: biology, chemistry, computer science, physics, earth sciences, engineering or a closely related subject. Applicants must be enrolled in a bachelor's programme at a North American, British or Irish university.

Deadline: 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm CET. Please verify all dates and requirements on the official DAAD portal before applying.
