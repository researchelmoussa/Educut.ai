Considering a master's or PhD in science or engineering? 🎓

King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in Thuwal, Saudi Arabia, is running its Fall 2027 admission for MS, MS/PhD and PhD programmes. All admitted MS and PhD students receive the KAUST Fellowship, which is fully funded and covers tuition, housing, health insurance and relocation support.

Field: science, technology, engineering and mathematics. The essential step is admission to a KAUST MS or PhD programme.

Round 2 deadline: 3 January 2027. KAUST describes its dates as tentative, so please verify all details on the official application portal before applying.
