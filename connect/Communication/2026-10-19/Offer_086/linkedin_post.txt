Early-career researchers with a connection to Denmark: this fellowship spans two research environments. 🔬

The Novo Nordisk Foundation's Postdoctoral Fellowships at Uppsala University - 2027 offer three years of research: two years at Uppsala University in Uppsala, Sweden, and a final year at a Danish institution.

Field: natural and technical sciences, biomedicine and biotechnology (interdisciplinary).

Key requirements:
- A PhD in one of these fields within 5 years of the fellowship start date
- A connection to Denmark and a host principal investigator at Uppsala University

Deadline: 29 January 2027, 2:00 pm Copenhagen time. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
