Aalto University Science Institute International Summer Research Programme
Aalto University Science Institute (AScI) | Espoo, Finland

A 12-week on-site research internship (June to August 2027) for bachelor's and master's students worldwide, and doctoral students in exceptional cases.

Applicants choose up to three projects. Projects are listed in mid-December, applications open 7 January 2027, and the deadline is 31 January 2027, 23:59 (UTC+2).

Please verify all details on the official Aalto application portal before applying.
