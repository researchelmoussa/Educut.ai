A professional development opportunity for early-career researchers in mathematics and computer science. 🎓

The Heidelberg Laureate Forum Foundation will accept applications from young researchers to attend the 14th Heidelberg Laureate Forum in Heidelberg, Germany, planned for 12–17 September 2027 (dates subject to change).

Who can apply: undergraduate/pre-master, PhD and postdoc researchers in mathematics and computer science.

📅 Application window: 11 November 2026 to 11 February 2027, 11:59 p.m. Berlin time. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
