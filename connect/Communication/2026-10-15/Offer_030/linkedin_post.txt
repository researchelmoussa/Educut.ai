Applying AI to a scientific problem? This fellowship in Singapore is built around that idea. 🔬

NTU AI-for-X Postdoctoral Fellowship at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

Field: AI applied to scientific problems (STEM). Proposals should integrate AI to solve a scientific problem.

Essentials: a PhD in a STEM field conferred in 2023 or later, and an NTU faculty member as Project Mentor. Fellows must start by 31 March 2027.

The official page lists up to 4 years of funding and an SGD 250,000 research grant.

Deadline: 30 October 2026, 23:59 Singapore time (UTC+8). Please confirm the details on the official page before applying.
