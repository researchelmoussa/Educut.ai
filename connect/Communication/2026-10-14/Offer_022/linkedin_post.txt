Applying for a Master's at EPFL? You can also ask to be considered for one of its excellence fellowships. 🎓

Master excellence fellowships
EPFL | Switzerland
Field: Master studies (all EPFL Master programmes)

The fellowship provides CHF 10,000 per semester, for up to 4 semesters. To apply, tick the fellowship box on your Master application in IS-Academia. Current EPFL Master students and Master holders are not eligible.

📅 Deadline: 15 December 2026. Only applications paid for and validated by then are considered.
