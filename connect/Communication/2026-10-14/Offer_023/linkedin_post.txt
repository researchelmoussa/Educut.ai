Planning a Master's or PhD at a Japanese university from April 2027? This scholarship's application window closes on 31 October. 🎓

Scholarship for Foreign Students
Honjo International Scholarship Foundation | Japan
Field: Graduate studies (Master and PhD) at Japanese universities

It is a non-repayable monthly stipend of ¥180,000 to ¥230,000, depending on programme length. It does not cover tuition or travel. Applicants must be non-Japanese citizens enrolled in or applying for a graduate programme starting April 2027, and need basic Japanese conversation, as interviews are in Japanese.

📅 Deadline: 31 October 2026 (Japan time)
