School pupils with an idea for a science or technology experiment can propose it for a NASA-supported test flight. 🚀

NASA TechRise Challenge
NASA | United States
Field: Science and technology experiments for test flights

This challenge is for school pupils in grades 6–12, working in teams, not for university students or professionals. A teacher or school employee submits the team's proposal through Future Engineers. Winners are expected in January 2027, with flights in summer 2027.

📅 Proposals due: 2 November 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific Time
