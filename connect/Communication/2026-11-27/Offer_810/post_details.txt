About: UNESCO-TWAS and FAPESP call for postdoctoral fellowships for researchers from TWAS-defined developing countries (Brazil excepted), hosted at institutions in São Paulo state. After a candidate-supervisor match, the supervisor submits the full proposal to FAPESP through SAGe.
Requirements:
- Native and permanent resident of a country classified by TWAS as developing, with the exception of Brazil
- Doctorate in an area of natural, applied or social sciences, applying within seven years of obtaining it
- Not already in Brazil and not holding a temporary or permanent residence visa in Brazil or in any developed country
- A matched supervisor in the state of São Paulo who submits the full proposal to FAPESP through SAGe
