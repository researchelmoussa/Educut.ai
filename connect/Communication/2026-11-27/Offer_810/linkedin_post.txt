UNESCO-TWAS/FAPESP Call for Proposals for Postdoctoral Fellowships for Researchers from Developing Countries
FAPESP and UNESCO-TWAS | Host institutions in São Paulo state, Brazil
Fields: natural, applied and social sciences

Eligible: natives and permanent residents of TWAS-defined developing countries (except Brazil), with a doctorate obtained within the last seven years.

Important: the supervisors' expression-of-interest stage closed on 1 November 2026. Candidates now need a matched supervisor in São Paulo state, who submits the full proposal to FAPESP through SAGe. Full-proposal deadline: 1 April 2027. Verify all terms in the official call.
