Doctoral research in business and economics, in Basel.

The Graduate School of Business and Economics (GSBE), University of Basel, in Basel, Switzerland, calls for applications to its PhD Program, with positions supervised by professors listed in each call. Calls are contingent on openings.

Applicants submit an application form, CV, degree diplomas with transcripts and a scientific writing sample through the University of Basel online portal, where an application fee applies. For a PhD starting in fall 2027, the deadline is 31 January 2027.
