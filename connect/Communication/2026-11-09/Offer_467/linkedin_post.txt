Planning an English-taught Master's in engineering, architecture or design in Italy?

Politecnico di Milano, in Milan, Italy, has published its call for Merit Based International Scholarships for academic year 2027-2028. They are for applicants to its English-taught Master of Science programmes, and all awards include a tuition fee waiver.

The admission application fee must be paid between 1 October and 1 December 2026. The scholarship form deadline is 15 December 2026 at 12:00 noon Italian time.

Please verify all details on the official Politecnico di Milano portal.
