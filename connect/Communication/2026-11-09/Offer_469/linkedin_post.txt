Considering coursework study at the University of Sydney as an international student?

The University of Sydney, in Sydney, Australia, runs the Vice-Chancellor's International Scholarships Scheme across all coursework fields, at undergraduate and postgraduate level. International students holding an unconditional offer by the round closing date are considered automatically, with no separate application.

Round 1 for Semester 2, 2027 entry closes on 15 February 2027, with later rounds in March and May 2027.

Dates and eligibility should be rechecked, so please verify all details on the official University of Sydney portal.
