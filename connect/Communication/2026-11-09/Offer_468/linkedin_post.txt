Recently passed your EPFL candidacy exam?

EPFL, in Lausanne, Switzerland, invites applications to the EPFL PhD Excellence Program, open to doctoral students in all EPFL fields. Applicants must have passed the candidacy exam within the last 12 months, or pass it before the programme starts in May 2027, and matriculated between 31 May 2025 and 31 May 2026.

Applications need a CV of up to two pages and a support letter from the thesis director. The deadline is 5 February 2027 at 17:00 Swiss time.

Please verify all details on the official portal.
