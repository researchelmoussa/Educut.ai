Planning a public policy master's? The Hertie School runs a scholarship programme for nationals of listed African countries.

African Scholars Programme | Hertie School
Berlin, Germany | Public policy, international affairs, data science for public policy

It is for applicants to the Master of Public Policy, Master of International Affairs or Master of Data Science and AI for Public Policy. Submit the master's application by the Early Bird deadline of 1 December 2026; funding applications open only after admission and need a short leadership essay.

The page gives this date without a year, so please verify it and the eligible countries on the official site.
