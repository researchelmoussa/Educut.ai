DAAD Artists-in-Berlin Program
DAAD Artists-in-Berlin Program (Berliner Künstlerprogramm des DAAD)
📍 Berlin, Germany
Fields: literature, music & sound, film

The 2028 residency fellowships run 12 months in literature and in music & sound, and 6 months in film. Open applications are accepted only in these three disciplines; visual arts candidates are nominated by a jury. Artists residing primarily in Germany are generally excluded.

Deadline: 15 December 2026, 23:59 CET
