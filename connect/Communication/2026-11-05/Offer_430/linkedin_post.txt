Planning an international master's in the Netherlands? Utrecht University offers a scholarship for non-EU/EEA students.

NL Scholarship
Utrecht University, Utrecht, Netherlands

Who it is for: non-EU/EEA students who have applied for an international master's programme at Utrecht University starting 1 September 2027. Not all programmes participate.

Deadline: 1 February 2027, 23:59 CET. Selection starts only after the deadline.

These details were found via search, so please verify eligibility and participating programmes on the official Utrecht University page before applying.
