About: Sciences Po's Émile Boutmy scholarship is for first-time, non-EU applicants admitted to an undergraduate or master's programme. Undergraduate applicants from foreign secondary schools must apply for the September 2027 intake by 13 January 2027; scholarship decisions follow by the end of April.
Requirements:
- First-time applicants to Sciences Po from a non-European Union state whose household does not file taxes within the EU
- Must be admitted to an undergraduate or master's programme
- Scholarship must be selected in the online application; no scholarship applications after submission of the Sciences Po application
