Planning to study at Sciences Po? Here is a scholarship for first-time applicants from outside the EU.

The Émile Boutmy scholarship | Sciences Po
Paris, France | Social sciences, humanities and public affairs

It is open to first-time applicants from a non-EU state whose household does not file taxes within the EU and who are admitted to an undergraduate or master's programme. The scholarship must be selected in the online application; requests are not accepted after submission.

For undergraduate applicants from foreign secondary schools, the September 2027 intake deadline is 13 January 2027. Master's dates were not confirmed, so please verify details on the official portal.
