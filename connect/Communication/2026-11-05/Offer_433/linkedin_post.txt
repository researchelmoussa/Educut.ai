Undergraduates in Asia with an interest in bioscience: the University of Tokyo lists an application period for its 2027 summer research programme.

UTokyo Amgen Scholars Program
The University of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan
Field: Bioscience and biotechnology

Open to undergraduates at bachelor's-awarding institutions across the Asia region who have completed at least their first year and will return to undergraduate study afterwards. Two recommenders are needed, one a faculty member at your current institution.

The online application period closes 1 February 2027, 3 p.m. JST.
