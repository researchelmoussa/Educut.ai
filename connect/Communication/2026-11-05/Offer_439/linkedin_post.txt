Young pianists and string players in the Asia-Pacific region: the Verbier Festival is holding masterclasses in Shenzhen.

Verbier Festival Shenzhen Masterclasses | Verbier Festival
Shenzhen, China | Classical music performance (piano, strings, chamber music)

Applications close 30 November 2026 at 23:59 China Standard Time. These details were found through search, so please verify them on the official festival page before applying.
