Natural science undergraduates outside Japan: the University of Tokyo School of Science runs a six-week research internship in summer 2027.

The University of Tokyo Research Internship Program
School of Science, The University of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan
Field: Natural sciences

Applicants must be enrolled in, and expected to return to, a bachelor's programme at a university outside Japan, studying a natural science or related field, and able to commit full-time to all six consecutive weeks.

Applications open 12 November 2026 and close 25 December 2026, 17:00 JST.
