Admitted to a master's at the University of Twente? A scholarship for excellent non-EU/EER students is accepting applications.

University of Twente Scholarship (UTS)
University of Twente, Enschede, Netherlands

Who it is for: excellent students from non-EU/EER countries who are (provisionally) admitted to a qualifying UT master's programme starting in 2027/2028. The scholarship application is separate from the programme application.

Deadline: 1 April 2027, 16:00 CET. Results are expected in early May 2027.
