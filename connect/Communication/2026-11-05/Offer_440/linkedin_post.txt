Researching international law? The Hague Academy of International Law has announced its 2027 Doctoral Scholarships.

Doctoral Scholarships | The Hague Academy of International Law
The Hague, Netherlands | International law

The scholarships are for a research stay in The Hague from 1 July to 1 September 2027. They are open to PhD candidates from non-OECD states residing in their home country who expect to defend their thesis by 31 December of the year after the award. A law degree (master's preferred) and proficiency in English or French are required.

Applications close 31 January 2027, 23:59 Hague time. Please verify details on the official page.
