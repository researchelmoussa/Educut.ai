UCL and the China Scholarship Council offer a joint scholarship route into doctoral research in London. 🎓

China Scholarship Council-UCL Joint Research Scholarship
University College London (UCL), London, United Kingdom
Field: All research areas (2027/28 entry)

Who it is for: prospective first-year PhD students who are citizens and permanent residents of the People's Republic of China at the time of application. Successful candidates then apply separately to the China Scholarship Council.

Deadline: 8 January 2027 for most subject areas; 13 January 2027 for Mathematical & Physical Sciences and IOE.
