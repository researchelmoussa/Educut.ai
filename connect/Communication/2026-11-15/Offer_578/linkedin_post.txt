Considering a PhD in Berlin? 🎓

Freie Universität Berlin is accepting applications for the FUB-CSC PhD Program 2027 in Berlin, Germany, covering all disciplines at the university.

Requirements include a Master's degree, a 2-3 page research statement and an English (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL iBT 95) or German (TestDaF 16 / DSH-2) language certificate. University applications close 15 January 2027.

Please note: accepted candidates then apply to the China Scholarship Council (CSC) separately, so CSC support is not confirmed at the admission stage.
