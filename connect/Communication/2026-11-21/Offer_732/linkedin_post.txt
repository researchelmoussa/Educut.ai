Early-career neuroscientists heading towards the IBRO World Congress may want to note this. 🧠

The International Brain Research Organization (IBRO) lists "IBRO World Congress Travel Grants", linked to the IBRO World Congress in Cape Town, South Africa, in the field of neuroscience.

Who can apply: PhD neuroscience students and early-career postdoctoral fellows (maximum 5 years from the start of the postdoc). Applicants must have submitted an abstract to the Congress as first author, with a valid abstract ID.

Applications open on 11 January 2027. Official IBRO pages give different deadlines (15 March and 17 March 2027), so please confirm the date and all other details on the official IBRO portal.
