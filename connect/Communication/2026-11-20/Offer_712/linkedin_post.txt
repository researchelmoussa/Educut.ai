Postdoctoral researchers working on forced migration: the Gerda Henkel Foundation has published its next deadline. 🌍

The Gerda Henkel Foundation's Forced Migration special programme covers research in the humanities and social sciences. The 2027 call funds only individual research scholarships for postdoctoral researchers.

Key requirements: a doctorate no more than eight years old at the time of application, and a digital application in English. No host city or country is specified.

Application deadline: 29 April 2027.
