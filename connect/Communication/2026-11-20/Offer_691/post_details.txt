About: The Wenner-Gren Foundation's Wadsworth African Fellowship supports African doctoral students in anthropology at South African universities, for fellowships beginning in the following academic year. The online portal opens 1 November and applications, with both sponsor letters, are due 15 December 2026.
Requirements:
- Citizens and residents of an African country at the time of application
- Members of a group underrepresented in academic anthropology and archaeology
- Doctoral application pending at a South African university, or in the first year of the doctoral programme
- Applicants seeking only fieldwork or dissertation write-up support are not eligible
- Two sponsor letters (home and host) must arrive by the deadline; application in English
