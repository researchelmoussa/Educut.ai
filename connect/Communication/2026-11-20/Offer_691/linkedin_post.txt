Early support can shape a doctoral path in anthropology and archaeology. 🌍

The Wenner-Gren Foundation lists the "Wadsworth African Fellowship" for doctoral study at universities in South Africa. Field: anthropology and archaeology.

Open to citizens and residents of an African country who belong to a group underrepresented in the field, with a doctoral application pending at a South African university or in the first year of the programme. Two sponsor letters must arrive by the deadline.

Deadline: 15 December 2026. The official page gives a recurring 15 December date, so please confirm the year and status on the official Wenner-Gren page before applying.
