Undergraduates interested in health research: a medical faculty in Canada is accepting applications.

Dalhousie University, Faculty of Medicine, in Halifax, Canada, accepts applications for its Undergraduate Summer Research Awards and Summer Student Research Program for Non‑Medical Students, in health and medical research.

Applicants should be at least in the second year of an undergraduate program, hold a GPA of at least 3.0 and have a supervisor whose primary appointment is in the Faculty of Medicine. Applications are due 15 January 2027, 5:00 pm AST.
