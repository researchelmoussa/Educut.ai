Global PhD in therapy innovation for lung cancer
Macquarie University | Sydney, Australia
Field: biomedical engineering / lung cancer therapy (boron neutron capture therapy)

This joint project with National Tsing Hua University is developing an inhaled boron neutron capture therapy platform. At this stage, prospective PhD students send an expression of interest by email to both supervisors. The preferred candidate is then invited to apply formally to both institutions.

Expressions of interest close on 31 January 2027. Please verify the details on the official Macquarie University page.
