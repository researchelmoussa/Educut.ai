Science journalists: one of the best-known mid-career programmes in the field has opened its next round.

Knight Science Journalism @MIT in Cambridge, Massachusetts, United States, accepts applications for the Knight Science Journalism Academic-Year Fellowship, 2027-28 class, open to professional journalists covering science.

Fellows must live in the Boston/Cambridge area during the 2027-28 academic year, and applicants cannot have completed a residential fellowship of four months or longer in the two years before applying. Applications close 9 January 2027, 11:59 pm ET.
