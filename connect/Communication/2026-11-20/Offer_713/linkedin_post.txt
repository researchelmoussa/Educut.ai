Researching urban history or the archaeology of vanished cities? This yearly call is aimed at research teams. 🏛️

The Gerda Henkel Foundation's Lost Cities special programme supports research in the humanities and social sciences on the urban history and archaeology of lost cities.

Key requirements: applicants must be postdoctoral researchers based at a university, and projects need a team of at least two scholars. Individual applications are not accepted. Applications are digital, in English or German. No host city or country is specified.

Next application deadline: 19 May 2027.
