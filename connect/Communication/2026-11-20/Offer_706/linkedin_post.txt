Researching South Asia from a university in Germany? MWF Delhi has opened a call for early-career writing scholarships. ✍️

The Max Weber Forum for South Asian Studies Delhi (MWF Delhi) in New Delhi, India, invites applications for "Early-career Writing Scholarships for Doctoral and Postdoctoral Scholars" in the humanities and social sciences with an emphasis on South Asia.

Eligibility: Germany-based doctoral students and postdoctoral scholars whose projects use primary sources with an emphasis on South Asia.

Deadline: 7 February 2027.
