Curious about building quantum technologies, not only studying them? ⚛️

The Institute for Quantum Computing, University of Waterloo, lists the "Undergraduate School on Experimental Quantum Information Processing 2027" in Waterloo, Canada. Field: quantum information processing.

It is aimed at undergraduate students; those finishing their degree right before the program may apply if they have not started a graduate program. International students are only considered if they also apply for a summer research Undergraduate Research Award (URA).

Deadline: 3 January 2027. Late applications will not be considered.
