Planning postgraduate study at the University of Cambridge? This scholarship is open to applicants across all subjects. 🎓

Gates Cambridge Scholarship
Gates Cambridge Trust, University of Cambridge | Cambridge, United Kingdom
Field: Postgraduate Study (all subjects)

Applicants must be applying for a postgraduate course at Cambridge for 2027/28 entry. The international round is for applicants other than US citizens resident in the US. Complete applications, including references, are due by your course's deadline.

📅 Deadline: 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027 (23:59 GMT), depending on your course
