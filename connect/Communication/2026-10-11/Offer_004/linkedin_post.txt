Planning postgraduate study at Cambridge? This scholarship is open across all subjects. 🎓

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship, offered by the Gates Cambridge Trust, University of Cambridge, supports postgraduate study in Cambridge, United Kingdom.

Applicants must be applying for a postgraduate course at Cambridge for 2027/28 entry. The international round is for applicants other than US citizens resident in the US.

Deadline: complete applications, including references, are due by 23:59 GMT on 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on your course.
