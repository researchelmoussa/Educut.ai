Working in radio astronomy? The National Radio Astronomy Observatory has opened applications for one of its postdoctoral programs. 🔭

The Jansky Fellowship is a postdoctoral fellowship offered by the National Radio Astronomy Observatory (NRAO) in the United States, in the field of Radio Astronomy.

The nominal start is September 2027, and no later than January 2028. Applications are submitted as a single PDF, and reference letters are due by November 11, 2026.

Application deadline: October 28, 2026, 11:59 pm EDT.
