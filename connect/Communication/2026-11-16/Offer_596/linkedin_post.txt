PhD researchers working on digital narrative: Bergen has published its call for a 2027 summer school.

2027 Digital Narrative PhD Summer School
University of Bergen, Center for Digital Narrative | Bergen, Norway

Field: Digital narrative, digital culture and media studies. This five-day school, themed 'Play: Theory and Practice in Digital Narrative', runs 31 May to 4 June 2027. Applicants must be enrolled in a doctoral programme with a dissertation related to digital narratives.

Deadline: 7 January 2027.
