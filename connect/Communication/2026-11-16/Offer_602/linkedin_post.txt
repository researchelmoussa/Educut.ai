Researchers in digital science: a research stay in Lyon, in collaboration with Inria.

2027-2028 Inria/Collegium Fellowship
Collegium de Lyon (Institut d'études avancées de Lyon) and Inria | Lyon, France
Field: Digital science and technology

The fellowship hosts one researcher in Lyon for 5 to 10 months. Applicants hold a professorship or equivalent research post at an institution outside France, with a project supported by an Inria team at the Lyon Saint-Etienne site.

Application deadline: 29 January 2027, 6 pm Paris time.
