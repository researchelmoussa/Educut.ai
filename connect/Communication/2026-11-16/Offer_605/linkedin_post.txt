PhD students: the University of Bergen has announced its 2027 summer research school, with an AI-focused course among the options.

Bergen Summer Research School 2027 (BSRS)
University of Bergen | Bergen, Norway
Field: Interdisciplinary PhD courses

BSRS runs 7-17 June 2027 with seven parallel PhD courses, including Moral economies of Artificial Intelligence. Participants choose one course. It is open to PhD students with a strong command of English.

Application deadline: 1 February 2027.
