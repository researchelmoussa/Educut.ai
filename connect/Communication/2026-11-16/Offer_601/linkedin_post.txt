Researching games and game culture? The Center for Digital Narrative at the University of Bergen has issued a call for papers for Nordic DiGRA 2027.

Nordic DiGRA 2027
University of Bergen, Center for Digital Narrative | Bergen, Norway
Field: Game studies

The conference theme is Game Culture Narratives, and it takes place June 9-11, 2027. The call invites full papers (5,000-7,000 words), extended abstracts and workshop proposals.

Submission deadline: January 24, 2027.
