Early-career classical musicians and composers: Banff Centre has announced its Chamber Music 2027 residency.

Chamber Music 2027
Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity | Banff, Canada
Field: Classical chamber music and composition

The residency runs July 5-17, 2027. It is open to classically trained musicians and composers aged 18+ who are in post-secondary or postgraduate study, or recent graduates. Strings, woodwind, brass, piano, percussion and composition applicants are welcome, and applicants must be available for the entire programme.

Application deadline: January 13, 2027, 11:59 p.m. Mountain Standard Time.
