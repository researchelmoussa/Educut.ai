Undergraduates curious about laboratory research: The Rockefeller University has announced its summer 2027 programme.

Chemers Neustein Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship Program (SURF)
The Rockefeller University | New York, United States
Field: Laboratory research at The Rockefeller University

SURF runs from 7 June to 13 August 2027 for college sophomores and juniors, and international students are eligible to apply. The application includes a personal statement, an official transcript, two recommendation letters and a CV.

Application deadline: 1 February 2027.
