Looking for computer science research experience next summer? 🔬

ETH Zurich, Department of Computer Science, offers the ETH Student Summer Research Fellowship in Zurich, Switzerland, from 2 July to 27 August 2027.

It is open to bachelor and master students in computer science or a closely related field with two years of study completed by the programme start. ETH Zurich students are not eligible.

Fellows receive a CHF 4,000 stipend for housing and living expenses; travel and visa costs are covered.

📅 Applications via SiROP open 1 November 2026 and close 16 December 2026, 13:00 CET. Please verify details on the official portal.
