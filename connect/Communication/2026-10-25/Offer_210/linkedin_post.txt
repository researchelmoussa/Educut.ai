India's three national science academies have opened a new cycle of their summer research fellowships. 🔬

The Science Academies' Summer Research Fellowship Programme for Students and Teachers 2027 is offered by the Indian Academy of Sciences, Indian National Science Academy and The National Academy of Sciences, India (IAS-INSA-NASI) in India, in science and engineering research.

It is open to students and teachers at institutions in India, and involves two months of research with an assigned guide, preferably in summer. The host city depends on the assigned guide.

Online applications close on 30 November 2026. Please verify every detail on the official portal before applying.
