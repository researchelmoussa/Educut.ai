A new doctoral call in applied mathematics has been published in Brazil.

Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), Programa de Pós-Graduação em Matemática Aplicada (PPGMAp) has announced its "Doutorado em Matemática Aplicada (seleção 2027/1)" selection.

Location: Brazil
Field: Applied mathematics

The call offers ten places, three reserved for the affirmative-action programme. Selection is a single stage based on a preliminary thesis project and academic document review.

Applications open on 3 November 2026 and close on 4 December 2026. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
