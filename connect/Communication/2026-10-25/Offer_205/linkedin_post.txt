Postdoctoral researchers in the mathematical sciences: the PIMS fellowship competition is open. 📐

The PIMS Postdoctoral Fellowship is offered by the Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences (PIMS) in Canada. The host city depends on the sponsoring member university.

Applications are by nomination only. Nominees need a sponsor at a PIMS member university, and all nominees apply through MathJobs.

Deadline for complete nominations and applications: 1 December 2026. Sponsor nomination forms are due 5 December 2026.

Please verify all details on the official PIMS portal before applying.
