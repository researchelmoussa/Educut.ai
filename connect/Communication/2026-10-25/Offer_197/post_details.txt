About: Two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Michigan applying AI in the sciences, engineering and mathematics, with one AI mentor and one science mentor. This is the final application cycle; the priority deadline is 23 November 2026, with starts in August or September 2027.
Requirements:
- PhD in any field, conferred before the fellowship start (August or September 2027) and received after 1 September 2022
- Two U-M faculty mentors for the full 2 years: one AI mentor and one science mentor; a prior or current faculty mentor is ineligible
- Biomedical proposals focused on disease diagnosis, prevention or treatment are out of scope
- Fellows must work in person on the University of Michigan campus
- Materials: CV, AI keywords, research tagline and blurb, research statement, Individualized Development Plan, Responsible AI statement, letters from both mentors
Skills: Artificial intelligence, Machine learning, Scientific research
