The Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society (MIDAS), University of Michigan, United States, is accepting applications for the Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellows Program.

Field: AI in the natural sciences, engineering and mathematics. Two years, pairing each fellow with one AI mentor and one science mentor.

Key requirements: a PhD received after 1 September 2022 and conferred before the fellowship start, and in-person work on the University of Michigan campus.

Priority deadline: 23 November 2026, 11:59 pm EST. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
