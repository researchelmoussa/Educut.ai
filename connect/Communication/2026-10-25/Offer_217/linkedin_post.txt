The Mandela Rhodes Scholarship, offered by The Mandela Rhodes Foundation, supports postgraduate study and leadership development for the Class of 2028.

Key requirements:
• African citizens aged 19 to 29
• Undergraduate degree completed by 1 January 2028

Applications open on 9 March 2027 and close on 14 April 2027 at 23:59 GMT+2. References must also be uploaded by the deadline.

These details were confirmed through search results for the official page, so please verify them on the official Mandela Rhodes Foundation portal before applying.
