Planning a PhD in Australia? 🎓 Adelaide University and the China Scholarship Council run a partnership scholarship for doctoral study.

China Scholarship Council - Adelaide University Partnership Scholarship Program
Adelaide University | Adelaide, Australia
Field: All research fields (PhD)

Key points:
- For PhD commencement between July 2027 and June 2028
- Requires an unconditional Adelaide offer that depends on also winning a CSC award

Applications to Adelaide run until 1 February 2027. Nominated candidates then apply to the CSC around April to May 2027.
