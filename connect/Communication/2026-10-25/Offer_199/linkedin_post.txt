A new fellowship call is open for researchers working on the physics of complex systems. 🔬

The Distinguished PKS Postdoctoral Fellowship is offered by the Max Planck Institute for the Physics of Complex Systems (MPI-PKS) in Germany. One fellowship is awarded per year: a two-year appointment with a possible one-year extension.

Applicants generally need at least one year of postdoctoral experience at an institution other than the one awarding their PhD. The application includes a research proposal and three reference letters.

Application deadline: 13 November 2026. Please verify all details on the official portal before applying.
