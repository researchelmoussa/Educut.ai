Planning a PhD in Australia? This joint scholarship route is worth a closer look. 🎓

The University of Queensland and the China Scholarship Council offer the CSC-UQ PhD Scholarship (China Scholarship Council), open across disciplines at doctoral level in Australia.

Key points from the official page:
- Applicants must first be offered a place in UQ's PhD program
- Expressions of Interest open Monday 2 November 2026
- Full applications are due 20 January 2027

Eligibility conditions such as nationality were not captured here, so please verify all details on the official UQ portal before applying.
