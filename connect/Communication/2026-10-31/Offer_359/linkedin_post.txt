Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowships (SURF) 2027
California Institute of Technology (Caltech), Student-Faculty Programs, United States
Field: Science and engineering research | Level: Undergraduate

SURF pairs undergraduates with Caltech mentors for summer research. Applicants need two confidential letters of recommendation, one from a faculty member. International students can take part on the Caltech campus; JPL placements are limited to U.S. citizens and permanent residents.

Deadline: February 22, 2027. Please verify current dates and details on the official Caltech portal before applying.
