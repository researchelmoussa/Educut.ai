Thinking about an undergraduate degree where mathematics meets technology and innovation?

IMPA Tech (Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada) has published the selection call for its Bacharelado em Matemática da Tecnologia e Inovação (turma 2027) in Brazil, with 150 places split between Rio de Janeiro and Teresina.

Field: mathematics, technology and innovation. Selection is based on medals in five science olympiads or the ENEM mathematics score (2024, 2025 or 2026 scores accepted), followed by a two-phase process.

Registration opens 3 November 2026 and closes 23 December 2026 at 12:00 (São Paulo time). Please verify all details on the official IMPA Tech portal before applying.
