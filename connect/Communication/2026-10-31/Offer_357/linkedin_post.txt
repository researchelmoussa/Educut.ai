Curious what a summer inside a genetics research lab could look like? 🧬

The Jackson Laboratory (JAX) has announced the JAX Summer Student Program 2027, a 10-week internship in genetics and biomedical research for high school and undergraduate students in the United States.

The program runs at Bar Harbor (residential) or Farmington (non-residential). Applications for 2027 open in November and must be formally submitted in the portal by January 25, 2027, at 5:00 p.m. ET. Acceptance notifications are sent by email by March 31.

Please verify dates and eligibility on the official JAX portal before applying.
