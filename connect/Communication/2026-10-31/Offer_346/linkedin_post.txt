Considering an undergraduate degree built around artificial intelligence? 🎓

Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in the United Arab Emirates is admitting students to its Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence (Fall 2027 intake).

The programme has two streams, Engineering and Business. Applicants may submit only one application per admission cycle and are invited to an online screening exam within 10 days of applying.

Regular Decision deadline: 1 February 2027, 5 p.m. GST. Please verify all dates and details on the official MBZUAI portal before applying.
