Five years or more in journalism? Harvard's Nieman Foundation is accepting applications for its international fellowship track.

Opportunity: Nieman Fellowship (International, 2027-28)
Institution: Nieman Foundation for Journalism at Harvard University
Location: Cambridge, United States
Field: Journalism

Who it is for: working journalists with at least five years of full-time media experience. The application includes two essays, a study proposal summary, work samples and three recommendation letters.

International application deadline: 1 December 2026.
