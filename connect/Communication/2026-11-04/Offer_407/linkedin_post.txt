How do nervous systems produce behavior? The Marine Biological Laboratory runs an advanced research training course built around that question.

Neural Systems & Behavior is offered by the Marine Biological Laboratory (MBL) in Woods Hole, United States, in the field of neuroscience (neural basis of behavior).

The course is aimed at graduate students, postdoctoral researchers and independent investigators, and is capped at 20 participants.

Application deadline: 2 February 2027 (US Eastern time).
