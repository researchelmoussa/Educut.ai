Applications for the European Master in Law and Economics (EMLE) 2027/2028 opened on 1 September 2026. Offered by the European Master in Law and Economics (EMLE) consortium, it is an Erasmus Mundus master's (LL.M.) delivered across partner universities.

About 15 full Erasmus+ scholarships are available per academic year. They are merit-based, and only fully completed applications with at least one letter of reference are considered.

Deadline: 31 December 2026 for non-European applicants (15 January 2027 for European applicants). Please verify all details on the official EMLE portal before applying.
