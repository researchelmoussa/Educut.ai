PhD funding for art history and artistic research, open to citizens of all countries. 🎨

The Novo Nordisk Foundation has opened the call for the Mads Øvlisen PhD Scholarships within Art History and Practice-Based Artistic Research 2027.

📍 Denmark (the project must primarily take place in Denmark)
Field: Art history; practice-based artistic and curatorial research
Funding: up to DKK 3,080,000 per three-year grant

Applicants need a completed master's degree or equivalent. Practice-based applicants also need a substantial, documented artistic or curatorial practice.

🗓️ Deadline: 14 January 2027, 2:00 pm Copenhagen time. Please verify details on the official portal.
