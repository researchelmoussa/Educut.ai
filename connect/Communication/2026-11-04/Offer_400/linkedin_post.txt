The 2027 call for the Novo Nordisk Foundation's Postdoctoral Fellowships at ETH Zurich (Zurich, Switzerland) supports interdisciplinary projects in natural and technical sciences, biomedicine or biotechnology with potential impact on human health or sustainability.

Key requirements: a PhD obtained within five years of the start date, a confirmed ETH Zurich host PI and a Danish host institution that employs the fellow.

Funding: up to DKK 3.6 million per fellowship.
Deadline: 29 January 2027, 2:00pm Copenhagen time.

Please verify all details on the official Novo Nordisk Foundation portal before applying.
