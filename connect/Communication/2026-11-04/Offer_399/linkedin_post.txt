Two years in Oxford, then a final year in Denmark: a postdoctoral route linking research in two countries.

The Novo Nordisk Foundation's "Postdoctoral Fellowships at University of Oxford - 2027" is a three-year fellowship based in Oxford, United Kingdom, in natural and technical sciences, biomedicine and biotechnology (interdisciplinary).

Key requirements:
- A PhD in these fields obtained within five years of the start date
- A research collaboration set up with a University of Oxford group

Deadline: 29 January 2027, 2:00pm (Copenhagen time).

Please verify all details on the official Foundation portal before applying.
