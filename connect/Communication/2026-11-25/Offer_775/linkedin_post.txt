Early career researchers working on violence against women and children: SVRI's 2027 fellowships are open.

SVRI Early Career Research Fellowships
Sexual Violence Research Initiative (SVRI)

Eligibility is limited to researchers in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs): doctoral or postdoctoral researchers who are nationals of an LMIC and enrolled full-time at an accredited LMIC institution. The focus is the intersection of violence against women and violence against children. There is no single host country.

Deadline: 15 January 2027, 11:59pm South African time.
