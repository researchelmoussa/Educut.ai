The AITHYRA International PhD Call in AI for Biomedicine (all genders)

AITHYRA · Vienna, Austria

Deadline 1 November 2026Deadline 1 November 2026

In short

AITHYRA invites applications to its International PhD Program in AI for Biomedicine in Vienna, Austria, run with TU Wien, Uni Wien and MedUni Wien. The program is for Master's graduates, or students finishing their Master's by September 2027, who want doctoral research at the meeting point of artificial intelligence and biomedicine. It starts on 1 September 2027.

  • Master's degree in a relevant field, or expected by September 2027.
  • Run with TU Wien, Uni Wien and MedUni Wien in Vienna.
  • The program starts on 1 September 2027.
  • Application window: 1 September to 1 November 2026.
  • Deadline: 1 November 2026.
