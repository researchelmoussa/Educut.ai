In short

EIPOD-LinC (Interdisciplinary Postdocs - Exploring Life in Context) is a postdoctoral fellowship programme of the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) in interdisciplinary life sciences. The 2026 call runs from 25 November 2026 to 1 February 2027, and applicants need a PhD by the deadline. Interviews take place on 8-9 April 2027, and fellows start by 1 September 2027 at the latest.

  • Postdoctoral fellowship in interdisciplinary life sciences at EMBL.
  • Applicants must hold a PhD by the application deadline.
  • The call opens on 25 November 2026; interviews are on 8-9 April 2027; latest start 1 September 2027.
  • Deadline: 1 February 2027, 23:59 (Europe/Berlin).
  • Status is likely open: confirm the dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
