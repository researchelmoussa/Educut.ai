Posters - CHI 2027

ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI 2027) · Pittsburgh, United States

Deadline 21 January 2027 (AoE)Deadline 21 January 2027 (AoE)

In short

CHI 2027, the ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, invites poster submissions for its meeting in Pittsburgh from 10 to 14 May 2027. Posters present human-computer interaction work in up to four single-column pages, excluding references. Submissions are anonymous, made through PCS and due on 21 January 2027 (AoE); notifications follow on 18 February 2027.

  • Up to four single-column pages, excluding references, submitted through PCS.
  • Submissions must be anonymous.
  • Posters are non-archival.
  • Notifications on 18 February 2027; the conference runs 10-14 May 2027 in Pittsburgh.
  • Deadline: 21 January 2027 (AoE). Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
