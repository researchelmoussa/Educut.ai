Assistant Professor Position in Solar Physics at the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan

National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ), Solar Science Observatory · Japan

Deadline 12 January 2027, 12:00 (Asia/Tokyo)Deadline 12 January 2027, 12:00 (Asia/Tokyo)

In short

The Solar Science Observatory of the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ) seeks an assistant professor in solar physics. The role promotes solar-stellar research using existing solar observation facilities and contributes to the performance evaluation of the SOLAR-C EUVST. The application status is not yet confirmed: verify all details, including eligibility and how to apply, on the official NAOJ announcement before applying.

  • Full-time, onsite assistant professor position at the NAOJ Solar Science Observatory in Japan.
  • Work focus: solar-stellar research with existing solar observation facilities and performance evaluation of the SOLAR-C EUVST.
  • Eligibility, required documents and how to apply are set out in the official NAOJ announcement.
  • Deadline: 12 January 2027, 12:00 JST, as given in the AAS SolarNews listing; the NAOJ announcement itself was not read.
