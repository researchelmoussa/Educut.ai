Early Career Fellowships

The Leverhulme Trust · United Kingdom

Deadline 18 February 2027, 16:00 (Europe/London)Deadline 18 February 2027, 16:00 (Europe/London)

In short

The Leverhulme Trust Early Career Fellowships support early-career researchers for 36 months at a UK host university. The 2027 round opens on 1 January 2027, and fellowships must start between 1 September 2027 and 1 May 2028. Applications are submitted through the host university.

  • For early-career researchers; fellowships last 36 months at a UK host university.
  • The doctoral thesis must have been submitted for viva voce examination not more than four years before the closing date.
  • Applications are submitted through the host university; the round opens on 1 January 2027.
  • The closing date is 18 February 2027, 16:00 (Europe/London).
  • Confirm that the call is open and check every date on the official portal before applying.
