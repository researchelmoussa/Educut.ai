Master's Programs (MP1, MP2), Admission 2027-2028

National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies (GRIPS) · Tokyo, Japan

Deadline 30 April 2027, 17:00 (Asia/Tokyo)Deadline 30 April 2027, 17:00 (Asia/Tokyo)

In short

The National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies (GRIPS) in Tokyo, Japan is accepting applications for 2027-2028 admission to its Master's Programs (MP1, MP2) in public policy, from applicants residing outside Japan. Applicants submit an Online Registration Form and then post a complete set of supporting documents. Details come from search results of the official page, so verify programme details and dates on the official GRIPS portal before applying.

  • For applicants residing outside Japan who seek a master's degree in public policy.
  • Online Registration Form deadline: 30 April 2027, 17:00 (Asia/Tokyo); supporting documents by post by 7 May 2027.
  • The Online Registration Form may be submitted only once per academic year; incomplete or late applications are not considered.
  • The extracted deadline chart was unclear and other dates may apply to other rounds; confirm on the official page.
  • Scholarship availability was not captured; check the official page.
