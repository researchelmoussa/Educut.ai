Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)

University of Toronto, Department of Computer Science · Toronto, Canada

Deadline 1 December 2026 (America/Toronto)Deadline 1 December 2026 (America/Toronto)

In short

The University of Toronto Department of Computer Science admits students to its Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programme in Toronto, Canada, for Fall 2027. It is a full-time, on-site programme. Applications go through the School of Graduate Studies; an application to the Vector Institute does not place candidates in the department's admissions pool.

  • For PhD applicants in computer science seeking Fall 2027 entry.
  • Applications are made through the University of Toronto School of Graduate Studies (SGS).
  • A Vector Institute application does not place candidates in the department's admissions pool.
  • The application deadline is 1 December 2026 (America/Toronto).
  • Confirm that the call is open and check every date on the official portal before applying.
