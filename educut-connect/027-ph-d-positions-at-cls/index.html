Ph.D. Positions at CLS

Max Planck ETH Center for Learning Systems (CLS) · Switzerland

Deadline 2 November 2026, 23:59 (Europe/Zurich)Deadline 2 November 2026, 23:59 (Europe/Zurich)

In short

The Max Planck ETH Center for Learning Systems (CLS), a collaboration between ETH Zurich and the Max Planck Society, offers funded PhD positions in machine learning, robotics and computer vision, starting in 2027. The positions are for Master's graduates who want to do doctoral research in learning systems.

  • A Master's degree is required; Bachelor's-only applicants are not eligible.
  • PhD positions start in 2027.
  • Apply with an online form, supporting documents and 2-3 reference letters.
  • Shortlisted candidates interview in Tübingen in March 2027.
  • Deadline: 2 November 2026, 23:59 (Europe/Zurich).
