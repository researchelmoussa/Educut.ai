Graduate Application, 2026-2027 Spring Semester (Graduate School of Social Sciences)

Middle East Technical University (METU), Graduate School of Social Sciences · Ankara, Turkey

Deadline 18 December 2026 (Europe/Istanbul)Deadline 18 December 2026 (Europe/Istanbul)

In short

The Graduate School of Social Sciences at Middle East Technical University (METU) in Ankara accepts applications to its master's and doctoral programmes for the 2026-2027 Spring semester from 23 November to 18 December 2026 (23:59). Results are announced on 22 January 2027.

  • Application window: 23 November to 18 December 2026, 23:59 (Turkey time).
  • Some programmes require the GRE or an equivalent; conditional admission may be possible without it.
  • International applicants: METU says their dates will be announced later, so this window may apply to domestic applicants only.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
