Postdoctoral Fellowships at HUJI

The Hebrew University of Jerusalem · Jerusalem, Israel

Deadline 31 January 2027 (Asia/Jerusalem)Deadline 31 January 2027 (Asia/Jerusalem)

In short

The Hebrew University of Jerusalem invites applications for 12-month postdoctoral fellowships for 2027-2028, open across a wide range of disciplines and starting in October 2027. The fellowship pays NIS 7,128 per month (subject to annual updates) plus a monthly housing allowance. Renewal for a further year requires a new application.

  • For new postdoctoral fellows only; current active HUJI postdoctoral fellows are not eligible.
  • Housing allowance: NIS 600 per month for single fellows or NIS 1,000 per month with family.
  • Further rules are set out in the university's postdoctoral regulations.
  • Deadline: 31 January 2027 (Asia/Jerusalem). Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
