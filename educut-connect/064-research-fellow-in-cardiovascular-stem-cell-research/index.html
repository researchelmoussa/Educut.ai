Research Fellow in Cardiovascular Stem Cell Research

Monash University · Clayton (Melbourne), Australia

Deadline 3 November 2026 (Australia/Melbourne)Deadline 3 November 2026 (Australia/Melbourne)

In short

A Research Fellow position at Monash University's Clayton campus in Melbourne, Australia, investigating the molecular and functional mechanisms underlying congenital heart defects. According to the aggregator, the work uses human stem cell-derived cardiomyocyte and cardiac organoid models with electrophysiology and genomics. For researchers in cardiovascular biology, stem cells or related fields.

  • Topic: mechanisms underlying congenital heart defects
  • Skills: stem cell-derived cardiomyocytes, cardiac organoids, electrophysiology and genomics
  • Location: Monash University Clayton campus, Melbourne
  • Contract: temporary Research Fellow position
  • Deadline: 3 November 2026 (Melbourne time)
