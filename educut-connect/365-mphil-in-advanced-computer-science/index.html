MPhil in Advanced Computer Science

University of Cambridge, Department of Computer Science and Technology · United Kingdom

Deadline 25 February 2027 (Europe/London)Deadline 25 February 2027 (Europe/London)

In short

The University of Cambridge's Department of Computer Science and Technology offers the nine-month MPhil in Advanced Computer Science, starting October 2027. Applications opened on 9 September 2026 and close on 25 February 2027, but applicants seeking University or Cambridge Trust funding must apply much earlier, by 2 December 2026. Applications are likely open; please verify dates and details on the official portal before applying.

  • Nine-month master's programme in computer science, starting October 2027
  • Funding deadline: apply by 23:59 GMT on 2 December 2026 for University and Cambridge Trust funding
  • Complete application materials must reach Postgraduate Admissions within ten days of the funding deadline to be considered for funding
  • Final application deadline: 25 February 2027 (Europe/London)
