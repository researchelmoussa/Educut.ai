Postdoctoral Researcher in Econometrics and Causal Inference

Deadline 15 December 2026 (Europe/Amsterdam)Deadline 15 December 2026 (Europe/Amsterdam)

In short

The University of Groningen's Department of Economics, Econometrics and Finance offers a three-year, full-time postdoctoral position on the project "Evaluation of Policy Interventions on Societal Inequality", supervised by Prof. Aico van Vuuren and Prof. Gerard J. van den Berg. The work centres on econometrics and causal inference for policy evaluation.

  • Three-year, full-time (1 FTE) position at the Faculty of Economics and Business in Groningen.
