AAMAS 2027 - Call for AAAI Fast Track

AAMAS 2027 (International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems) · Hanoi, Vietnam

Deadline 11 December 2026 AoE (UTC-12)Deadline 11 December 2026 AoE (UTC-12)

In short

A fast-track route into AAMAS 2027, held 3-7 May 2027 in Hanoi, Vietnam, for multiagent-systems papers that were not accepted at the AAAI-27 Main Technical Track and meet the review-score threshold. Decisions are expected on 1 February 2027.

  • The paper must have been submitted to the AAAI-27 Main Technical Track and not accepted.
  • The paper must be relevant to the AAMAS community; a review-score threshold applies, with differing wording in the call.
  • Upload a single PDF through the AAAI Track submission portal.
  • Deadline: 11 December 2026 AoE (UTC-12).
  • Application status not independently confirmed: verify dates and requirements on the official portal before applying.
