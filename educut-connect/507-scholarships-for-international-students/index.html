Scholarships for International Students

Graduate School of Yeungnam University · South Korea

Deadline 11 December 2026, 17:00 (Asia/Seoul)Deadline 11 December 2026, 17:00 (Asia/Seoul)

In short

The Graduate School of Yeungnam University in South Korea offers scholarships to new international master's and doctoral students in all graduate fields. Awards are tied to graduate admission, graded by language test scores and given on department recommendation. The second application round for Spring 2027 entry opens online on 16 November 2026, so the portal may not accept applications before then. Award amounts were not captured; verify all details on the official Yeungnam admissions page.

  • For new international graduate students recommended by their department.
  • Award tier is based on TOPIK, IELTS, TOEFL iBT or TOEIC scores.
  • Top-tier recipients assist with teaching or lab work up to 10 hours per week.
  • Online applications open 16 November 2026; documents are due 18 December 2026.
  • Deadline: 11 December 2026, 17:00 (Asia/Seoul; time zone assumed).
