Excellence Research Internship Program

EPFL · Lausanne, Switzerland

Deadline 30 November 2026 (Europe/Zurich)Deadline 30 November 2026 (Europe/Zurich)

In short

EPFL's Excellence Research Internship Program (ERIP) offers summer 2027 research internships in EPFL labs in Lausanne, Switzerland, in engineering, computer and basic sciences. It is for top-ranked Bachelor's and Master's students from EPFL partner universities in North America and Singapore. Shortlisting decisions follow in December. Any stipend or allowance is not listed here.

  • Only for students at EPFL partner universities in North America and Singapore.
  • Applicants must be among the top 10% of students at Bachelor's or Master's level.
  • Only complete applications are considered; late applications are not accepted.
  • Deadline: 30 November 2026 (Europe/Zurich); time of day not stated.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
