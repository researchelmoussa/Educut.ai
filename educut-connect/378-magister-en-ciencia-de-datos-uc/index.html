Magíster en Ciencia de Datos UC

In short

Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile (UC Online) offers the Magíster en Ciencia de Datos UC, an online Master's in Data Science taught over Zoom. The programme comprises 1,800 hours over 10 two-month terms. Applications close on 31 January 2027 and classes start on 22 March 2027. Applications are likely open; please verify dates and details on the official portal before applying.

  • Online Master's taught over Zoom: 1,800 hours across 10 two-month terms
  • Classes start on 22 March 2027
  • Incomplete applications are not processed, per the UC admissions page
  • Deadline: 31 January 2027 (America/Santiago)
