Associates Programme

The Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) · Trieste, Italy

Deadline 31 January 2027 (CET)Deadline 31 January 2027 (CET)

In short

ICTP in Trieste, Italy, runs an Associates Programme that supports scientists working in developing countries through research visits to the centre. Regular Associates are aged 36 to 45 and Senior Associates 46 to 65; both need a PhD and a main affiliation in a developing country. Applications close on 31 January 2027, for visits from 2028. Confirm the call on ICTP's official page before applying.

  • Regular Associates: scientists aged 36 to 45 working in developing countries
  • Senior Associates: scientists aged 46 to 65 working in a developing country
  • PhD and main affiliation in a developing country required
  • Apply through ICTP's online system by 31 January 2027; visits to ICTP can start in 2028
