Becas Internas Postdoctorales (Convocatoria 2026)

Consejo Nacional de Investigaciones Científicas y Técnicas (CONICET) · Argentina

Deadline 5 March 2027 (America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires)Deadline 5 March 2027 (America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires)

In short

CONICET's internal postdoctoral fellowships (Convocatoria 2026) offer 800 three-year research fellowships in Argentina across all scientific and technological disciplines, starting from 1 August 2027. Electronic applications run from 18 February to 5 March 2027, but each applicant's closing date depends on the last digit of their national ID (DNI), and 5 March is the latest. Check the official call for your exact date before applying.

  • For applicants who have submitted the final copies of their doctoral thesis, or defended it, when applying
  • Applications 18 February to 5 March 2027; closing date depends on the last DNI digit (ending 0 closes 1 March)
  • 800 postdoctoral fellowships; 70% for projects in Priority Topics and priority regions or areas
  • Fellowships start from 1 August 2027 and last 36 months
