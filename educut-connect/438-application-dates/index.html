In short

Koç University's Graduate School of Health Sciences in Istanbul accepts master's and doctoral applications for spring 2027 entry. Koç states that all admitted PhD students receive a full scholarship for the duration of their studies; the programmes open for this intake are listed on the official page.

  • Deadline: 5 December 2026 (Istanbul time).
  • Final admission decisions are notified 4-6 weeks after the application deadline.
  • The full scholarship statement covers PhD students; master's funding is not stated.
  • One general Koç page lists a spring intake only for another graduate school: confirm this intake on the official page.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
