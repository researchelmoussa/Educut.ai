Fields Postdoctoral Fellowships

Fields Institute for Research in Mathematical Sciences · Canada

Deadline 15 December 2026Deadline 15 December 2026

In short

The Fields Institute for Research in Mathematical Sciences offers six-month postdoctoral fellowships attached to its thematic programs; for 2027-2028 these include Geometric Analysis and Mathematical Physics, and Complex and Algebraic Dynamics in One Dimension. The stipend is $35,000 per six-month term. Applications go through MathJobs; the 15 December 2026 deadline is inferred from the standing annual 15 December date, so please verify on the official portal.

  • Six-month fellowships for participation in one of the Institute's major thematic programs
  • 2027-2028 programs: Geometric Analysis and Mathematical Physics (July-December 2027); Complex and Algebraic Dynamics in One Dimension (January-June 2028)
  • Apply via MathJobs; program organizers review applications and recommend candidates to the Fields Director
  • Deadline: 15 December 2026 (recurring annual date, year inferred); verify on the official portal
