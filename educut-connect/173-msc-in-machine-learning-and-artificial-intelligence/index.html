MSc in Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence

Stellenbosch University · Stellenbosch, South Africa

In short

Stellenbosch University in South Africa offers a one-year MSc in Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence and accepts applications for the January 2027 intake until 31 October 2026. Google DeepMind scholarships for students from Africa, administered by IIE, were tied to applications submitted by 15 August 2026.

  • One-year master's programme in machine learning and artificial intelligence, starting January 2027.
  • Admission requirements are listed on the programme's "How to apply" page.
  • Google DeepMind Scholarships are for students from Africa and were linked to the 15 August 2026 application date.
  • Deadline: 31 October 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg). Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
