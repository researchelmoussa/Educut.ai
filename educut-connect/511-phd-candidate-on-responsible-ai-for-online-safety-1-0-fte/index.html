In short

A full-time (1.0 FTE) PhD position at the Leiden Institute of Advanced Computer Science (LIACS), Leiden University, in its System and Security cluster. The research covers responsible AI for online safety, at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity and computational social science. The first contract is for one year, extended by three years after a positive evaluation; the expected start is March 2027. Check the official vacancy for salary, and verify all details on the official portal before applying.
