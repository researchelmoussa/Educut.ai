Doctorado en Ciencia y Tecnología de Inteligencia Artificial y Ciencia de Datos

Deadline 16 April 2027Deadline 16 April 2027

In short

IPN's doctorate in the science and technology of artificial intelligence and data science in Mexico is taught at five IPN sites, including CIDETEC. For the B27 round, starting August 2027, applicant registration runs from 7 September 2026 to 16 April 2027. Selection uses a GRE or EXANI-III score, English level and an interview. Requirements come from the previous call and may change, so verify them on the official admissions page.

  • Aimed mainly at holders of a master's in computing, engineering, exact sciences or related fields, especially AI and data science.
  • Selection (B26 call): GRE or EXANI-III score, English proficiency, an interview with the admission committee and the CV.
  • Offered at five IPN sites: CIC, CIDETEC, ESCOM, ESFM and UPIITA.
  • Registration deadline: 16 April 2027.
