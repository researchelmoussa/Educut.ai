International Teaching & Learning Conference 2027

In short

The University of South Africa (UNISA) calls for abstracts for its International Teaching & Learning Conference 2027, 3-6 May 2027 in Pretoria, South Africa, on teaching and learning in higher education. Participants register on the conference portal before submitting an abstract. Status is likely open: verify all details on the official portal before submitting.

  • Conference: 3-6 May 2027, Pretoria, South Africa.
  • Register on the portal first, then submit your abstract.
  • Full papers for a Progressio special edition are due 30 June 2027.
  • Deadline: 31 January 2027 (Africa/Johannesburg), 23:59 SAST, for abstracts.
