Call for applications to the 42nd cycle of PhD programmes

Politecnico di Torino · Torino, Italy

Deadline 16 November 2026, 14:00 (CET)Deadline 16 November 2026, 14:00 (CET)

In short

The second session of Politecnico di Torino's call for its 42nd cycle of PhD programmes in engineering, architecture and related fields, in Torino, Italy. Applications open on 15 October 2026 for programmes starting on 1 March 2027. The call offers a minimum of 110 positions with scholarships across three sessions.

  • For PhD applicants in engineering, architecture and related PhD programmes.
  • Second-session applications open on 15 October 2026.
  • PhD programmes start on 1 March 2027.
  • A minimum of 110 positions with scholarships across the call's three sessions.
  • Application deadline: 16 November 2026, 14:00 (CET).
