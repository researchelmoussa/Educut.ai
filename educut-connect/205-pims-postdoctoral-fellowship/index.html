PIMS Postdoctoral Fellowship

Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences (PIMS) · Canada

Deadline 1 December 2026Deadline 1 December 2026

In short

The PIMS Postdoctoral Fellowship competition supports postdoctoral researchers in the mathematical sciences at member universities of the Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences in Canada. Candidates are nominated by a sponsor at a PIMS member university. Complete nominations and applications are due 1 December 2026 via MathJobs, sponsor forms by 5 December, and decisions come by the end of March.

  • Nomination only; unsolicited applications are not considered
  • Nominees need a sponsor at a PIMS member university
  • All nominees apply through MathJobs
  • Applications due 1 December 2026; sponsor nomination forms due 5 December 2026
  • Application status not yet fully confirmed: confirm current status on the official page before applying
