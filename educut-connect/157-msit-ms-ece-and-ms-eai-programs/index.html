MSIT, MS ECE and MS EAI programs

Carnegie Mellon University Africa · Kigali, Rwanda

Deadline 15 January 2027Deadline 15 January 2027

In short

Carnegie Mellon University Africa in Kigali, Rwanda, offers three Master's programmes for fall 2027: the MS in Information Technology (MSIT), the MS in Electrical and Computer Engineering (MS ECE) and the MS in Engineering Artificial Intelligence (MS EAI). The early deadline is 15 December and applications officially close on 15 January 2027.

  • Bachelor's degree, or in the final year of a bachelor's programme; no minimum GPA.
  • Online application, written and video essays, CV, three recommenders and unofficial transcripts.
  • DET fees covered for first-time applicants who apply by the 15 December early deadline.
  • Deadline: 15 January 2027. Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
