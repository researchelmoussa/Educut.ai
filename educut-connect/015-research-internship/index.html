Research Internship

Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) · Japan

In short

A research internship at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) in Japan for later-year bachelor's and master's students. This Spring 2027 round offers internships of 4 to 6 months between 1 April and 30 September 2027 in science and technology research.

  • Open to later-year bachelor's and master's students interested in science and technology research.
  • Internships last 4 to 6 months, between 1 April and 30 September 2027.
  • Recommendation letters from professorial-level faculty are required, due the same day as the application.
  • Application deadline: 15 October 2026, 23:59 (JST).
