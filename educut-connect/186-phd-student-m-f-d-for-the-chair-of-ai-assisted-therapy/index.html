PhD Student (M/F/D) for the Chair of AI-Assisted Therapy Decisions

LMU Munich, Chair of AI-Assisted Therapy Decisions · Munich, Germany

Deadline 30 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin)Deadline 30 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin)

In short

A three-year PhD position at LMU Munich's Chair of AI-Assisted Therapy Decisions, led by MCML PI Björn Eskofier, in Munich, Germany. The PhD student works in AIM-ICU, a BMFTR-funded project on monitoring and treatment insights in intensive care, using machine learning for intensive care medicine. The position starts as soon as possible.

  • Excellent Master's degree or equivalent in computer science, artificial intelligence or a closely related field.
  • Strong background in machine learning and deep learning, Python and PyTorch; excellent English.
  • Desirable: experience with longitudinal data, NLP, transformers or clinical data analysis.
  • Apply by e-mail with one English PDF (CV, transcripts, reference letters, motivation letter) to the contact named in the posting.
  • Deadline: 30 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin). Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official listing before applying.
