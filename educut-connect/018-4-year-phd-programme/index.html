4-Year PhD Programme

Wellcome Sanger Institute · Cambridge, United Kingdom

Deadline 24 November 2026, 09:00 (GMT)Deadline 24 November 2026, 09:00 (GMT)

In short

The Wellcome Sanger Institute's 4-Year PhD Programme in Cambridge, United Kingdom, is open for its 2027 intake in genomics. It offers 12 funded studentships across the Institute's research areas. Funded students receive full support, including University tuition fees, whatever their nationality. Successful applicants also apply to the University of Cambridge.

  • Four-year doctoral programme in genomics at the Wellcome Sanger Institute, 2027 intake.
  • 12 funded studentships available across all of the Institute's research areas.
  • Funding includes University tuition fees and is open to applicants of any nationality.
  • Successful applicants also apply to the University of Cambridge.
  • Application deadline: 24 November 2026, 09:00 (GMT).
