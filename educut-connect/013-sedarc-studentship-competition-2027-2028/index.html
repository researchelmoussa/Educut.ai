SEDarc Studentship Competition 2027-2028

SEDarc · United Kingdom

Deadline 29 January 2027, 16:00 (GMT)Deadline 29 January 2027, 16:00 (GMT)

In short

The SEDarc Studentship Competition 2027-2028 offers ESRC-funded doctoral studentships in the social sciences through the SEDarc partnership, for 2027 entry in the United Kingdom. It is open to UK and international students, with UK home fees and a stipend. Applicants must first be nominated by one of six partner universities, each with its own internal deadline.

  • Open to UK and international students applying for a social sciences PhD.
  • ESRC-funded studentships for 2027 entry, with UK home fees and a stipend.
  • Applicants need a nomination from one of six SEDarc partner universities.
  • Each partner university sets its own internal deadline, earlier than the SEDarc deadline.
  • SEDarc deadline: 29 January 2027 at 16:00 GMT.
