Mechano-Genomics and AI

EMBO · Baden, Switzerland

Deadline 1 February 2027Deadline 1 February 2027

In short

EMBO Workshop "Mechano-Genomics and AI" takes place in Baden, Switzerland, from 3 to 5 May 2027, for researchers in mechanobiology, genomics and artificial intelligence, with an emphasis on junior researchers. Registration and abstract submission go through the EMBO meetings website. Participants are selected, and registration is confirmed only after selection and payment. Selected participants are notified by 15 February 2027. The application status is not yet confirmed: verify all details on the official portal before applying.

  • For researchers in mechanobiology, genomics and AI; the event page emphasises participation by junior researchers.
  • Workshop dates: 3 to 5 May 2027, onsite in Baden, Switzerland.
  • Submit registration and an abstract through the EMBO event page; participants are selected.
  • Selected participants are notified by 15 February 2027; registration is confirmed after selection and payment.
  • Deadline: 1 February 2027 (registration and abstract submission).
