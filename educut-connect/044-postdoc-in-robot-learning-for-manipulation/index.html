Postdoc in Robot Learning for Manipulation

KTH Royal Institute of Technology · Stockholm, Sweden

Deadline 1 November 2026 (Europe/Stockholm)Deadline 1 November 2026 (Europe/Stockholm)

In short

KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, Sweden, lists two full-time postdoctoral positions in robot learning for manipulation at its School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. The work focuses on Vision-Language-Action models for tabletop, mobile or dexterous manipulation, including whole-body motion and multi-fingered hands. Each position is temporary, for at most two years.

  • Two full-time, temporary postdoctoral positions of at most two years.
  • Doctorate required, in place by the time of the hiring decision.
  • Experience in at least two of: deep learning, reinforcement learning, imitation learning, robot perception, navigation, grasping, dexterous or mobile manipulation.
  • Research focus: Vision-Language-Action models for robotic manipulation.
  • Deadline: 1 November 2026 (Europe/Stockholm).
