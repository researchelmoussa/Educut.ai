CDDRL Pre-doctoral and Postdoctoral Fellowships

Center on Democracy, Development and the Rule of Law, Stanford University · Stanford, United States

Deadline 30 November 2026Deadline 30 November 2026

In short

Nine-month residential fellowships at Stanford University, from September to June, for pre-doctoral and postdoctoral scholars working on democracy, development and the rule of law. Fellows spend the year in residence at the Center on Democracy, Development and the Rule of Law, writing and doing research. CDDRL offers two to four fully funded fellowships each academic year.

  • Pre-doctoral applicants must be enrolled in a doctoral program and at the dissertation write-up stage through the residency period.
  • Postdoctoral applicants need a PhD earned within three years of the fellowship start, or a dissertation defense by July 31, 2027.
  • Onsite in Stanford, United States, for nine months, September to June.
  • Research and academic writing are the core skills for this fellowship.
  • Applications close on 30 November 2026.
