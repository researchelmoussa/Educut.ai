The Émile Boutmy scholarship

Sciences Po · Paris, France

Deadline 13 January 2027Deadline 13 January 2027

In short

Sciences Po's Émile Boutmy scholarship is for first-time, non-EU applicants admitted to an undergraduate or master's programme in Paris. Undergraduate applicants from foreign secondary schools must apply for the September 2027 intake by the scholarship deadline; scholarship decisions follow by the end of April.

  • First-time applicants from a non-EU state whose household does not file taxes within the EU.
  • You must be admitted to an undergraduate or master's programme.
  • Select the scholarship in the online application; it cannot be requested after you submit.
  • Deadline: 13 January 2027 for undergraduate applicants from foreign secondary schools; master's dates are on the official page.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
