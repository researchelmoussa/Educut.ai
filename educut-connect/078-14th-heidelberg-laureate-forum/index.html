14th Heidelberg Laureate Forum

Heidelberg Laureate Forum Foundation · Heidelberg, Germany

Deadline 11 February 2027, 23:59 (Europe/Berlin)Deadline 11 February 2027, 23:59 (Europe/Berlin)

In short

Young researchers in mathematics and computer science can apply to attend the 14th Heidelberg Laureate Forum in Heidelberg, Germany, from 12 to 17 September 2027 (dates subject to change). The Heidelberg Laureate Forum Foundation accepts applications from undergraduate or pre-master students, PhD candidates and postdoctoral researchers.

  • For undergraduate/pre-master students, PhD candidates and postdoctoral researchers in mathematics and computer science.
  • The forum takes place in Heidelberg, Germany, from 12 to 17 September 2027, subject to change.
  • The application window runs from 11 November 2026 to 11 February 2027.
  • Applications close on 11 February 2027, 23:59 (Europe/Berlin).
  • Confirm that the call is open and check every date on the official portal before applying.
