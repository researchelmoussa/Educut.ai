Oxford–UBS Centre for Applied AI DPhil Studentship: Future AI Systems for Finance

University of Oxford, Department of Engineering Science · Oxford, United Kingdom

Deadline 2 December 2026 (Europe/London)Deadline 2 December 2026 (Europe/London)

In short

A DPhil studentship at the University of Oxford's Department of Engineering Science for October 2027 entry, supervised by Professor Noa Zilberman within the Oxford–UBS Centre for Applied AI. The research covers hardware-software co-design, novel accelerators and resilient compute infrastructure for financial AI. One award is for a Home student and one for an Overseas student.

  • Strong undergraduate degree in Engineering, Computer Science or a related field, with a strong computational or engineering background.
  • Applicants must meet the University of Oxford doctoral admission requirements and quote reference 27ENGIN_NZ.
  • Deadline: noon on 2 December 2026 (UK time), for October 2027 entry.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
