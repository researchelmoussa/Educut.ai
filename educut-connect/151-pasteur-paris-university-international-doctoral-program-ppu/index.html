Pasteur-Paris University International doctoral program (PPU)

Institut Pasteur · Paris, France

Deadline 16 November 2026 (Europe/Paris)Deadline 16 November 2026 (Europe/Paris)

In short

Institut Pasteur and Paris universities run the Pasteur-Paris University International doctoral program (PPU) in biomedical and life sciences, with about 15 openings a year on three-year contracts. Candidates are first chosen by host labs, then reviewed by a selection committee. The call for October 2027 enrolment runs from 1 October to 16 November 2026, with results by mid-February.

  • Full-time PhD at Institut Pasteur in Paris: about 15 openings a year, three-year contracts.
  • Master's degree completed by the summer before enrolment; not required at the deadline.
  • Host labs choose candidates first; shortlisted candidates are interviewed in early February.
  • Deadline: 16 November 2026. Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
