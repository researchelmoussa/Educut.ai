Beca Avanza 2027-10

Universidad de los Andes, Facultad de Ingeniería · Colombia

Deadline 26 November 2026Deadline 26 November 2026

In short

Beca Avanza 2027-10 from the Faculty of Engineering at Universidad de los Andes, Colombia, supports students entering an in-person research master's. It covers tuition for up to eight credits per semester plus a research-monitor payment of COP 2,729,376 for 8 hours a week, for up to four semesters. There is one award per programme. Applications close on 26 November 2026, with results on 10 December; verify on the official page before applying.

  • For applicants admitted to an in-person engineering research master's for 2027-10, or undergraduate degree candidates.
  • Not open to holders of another scholarship or tuition funding, or students already active in such a master's at Uniandes.
  • Submit a motivation letter, undergraduate GPA evidence and evidence of prior contact with a faculty member or research group director.
  • Renewal requires a cumulative average of at least 4.0; nine scholarships in total.
  • Deadline: 26 November 2026.
