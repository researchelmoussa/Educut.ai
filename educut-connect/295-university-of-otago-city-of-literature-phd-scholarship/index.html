University of Otago City of Literature PhD Scholarship

University of Otago · Dunedin, New Zealand

Deadline 31 January 2027 (Pacific/Auckland)Deadline 31 January 2027 (Pacific/Auckland)

In short

The University of Otago offers one PhD scholarship each year for research on a topic relevant to literature, marking Dunedin's status as a UNESCO City of Literature. It provides a NZ$34,128 stipend per year plus a domestic tuition fee waiver (the student services fee is excluded). Applications close 31 January each year; the 2027 date is inferred from this annual deadline, so please verify on the official portal.

  • Open to graduates in any discipline planning PhD research on a topic relevant to literature
  • Applicants must be seeking their first doctoral qualification
  • One PhD candidate supported each year; applications are reviewed after 31 January
  • Eligibility of international students is not explicit; check the scholarship terms on the official page
  • Deadline: 31 January 2027 (recurring annual date, year inferred); verify on the official portal
