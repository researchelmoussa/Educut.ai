PhD in Mathematics in Natural, Social and Life Sciences

In short

Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI) in L'Aquila, Italy, has opened the 43rd-cycle call (academic year 2027/2028) for its full-time PhD programme in Mathematics in Natural, Social and Life Sciences. Applications are made online through the GSSI website only. A second round is held only if positions remain after the first. Eligibility details and scholarship amounts are in the full call.

  • Requires a Master's degree or equivalent; check the full call for the exact eligibility rules.
  • Applications are accepted only online through the GSSI website.
  • Deadline: 12 January 2027, 15:00 (Europe/Rome), first round; a second round runs only if positions remain.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
