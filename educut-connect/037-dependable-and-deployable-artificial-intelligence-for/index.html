Dependable and Deployable Artificial Intelligence for Robotics - PhD

UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training in Dependable and Deployable AI for Robotics (CDT-D2AIR) · Edinburgh, United Kingdom

Deadline 27 November 2026 (Europe/London)Deadline 27 November 2026 (Europe/London)

In short

A four-year, full-time PhD programme in dependable and deployable AI for robotics at the UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT-D2AIR) in Edinburgh, United Kingdom, offered jointly by Heriot-Watt University and the University of Edinburgh. Study starts in September 2027 and is in person only. Students define their PhD project and supervisory team during the first year.

  • Four-year PhD starting September 2027, jointly offered by Heriot-Watt University and the University of Edinburgh.
  • Full-time, in-person study only, in Edinburgh, United Kingdom.
  • The PhD project and supervisory team are defined during Year 1.
  • All reference letters must be received by the application deadline.
  • Deadline: 27 November 2026 (Europe/London); applications received by then are considered in the first round.
