22nd Singapore Graduate Forum on Southeast Asian Studies

Asia Research Institute, National University of Singapore · Singapore

Deadline 4 December 2026 (Asia/Singapore)Deadline 4 December 2026 (Asia/Singapore)

In short

The Asia Research Institute, National University of Singapore invites PhD students who have completed fieldwork and recent PhD recipients to the 22nd Singapore Graduate Forum on Southeast Asian Studies. The forum runs online on 7-11 June 2027 and at NUS in Singapore on 14-18 June 2027. Details come from search results of the official page, so verify them on the official portal before applying.

  • Open to PhD students who have completed fieldwork and to recent PhD recipients (awarded 2025 or later).
  • Apply with a 300-400 word abstract and a recommendation letter from your thesis supervisor or a senior scholar.
  • Selected participants submit a 2,000-3,000 word draft paper by 31 May 2027; results are expected by mid-February 2027.
  • Deadline: 4 December 2026 (Asia/Singapore); the time of day is not stated.
  • Funding or travel support for participants was not captured; check the official page.
