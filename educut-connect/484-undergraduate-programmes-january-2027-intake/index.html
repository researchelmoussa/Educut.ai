Undergraduate Programmes – January 2027 Intake

African Leadership University (ALU) · Kigali, Rwanda

Deadline 30 November 2026 (Africa/Kigali)Deadline 30 November 2026 (Africa/Kigali)

In short

African Leadership University (ALU Rwanda) in Kigali is accepting applications for its January 2027 undergraduate intake, including the Entrepreneurial Leadership and Software Engineering bachelor's tracks. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and seats for the preferred intake are limited. This is a self-funded degree admission, not a scholarship; tuition and financial aid are not listed here.

  • Bachelor's tracks in this intake include Entrepreneurial Leadership and Software Engineering.
  • Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; seats for the preferred intake are limited.
  • Deadline: 30 November 2026 (Africa/Kigali), for the January 2027 intake.
  • Some ALU pages show conflicting dates for other tracks; confirm the date for your track before applying.
  • Application status not yet confirmed on the official portal: verify every detail there before applying.
