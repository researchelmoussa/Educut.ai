Master of Science in Tropical Biodiversity and Ecosystems (Mundus tropicalis c/o TROPIMUNDO)

VLIR-UOS

Deadline 30 November 2026Deadline 30 November 2026

In short

VLIR-UOS offers full ICP Connect scholarships for the Mundus tropicalis (TROPIMUNDO) Master of Science in Tropical Biodiversity and Ecosystems, running from September 2027 to September 2029. The scholarship covers tuition fees, travel, insurance and living expenses (board and lodging). Applications opened 3 October 2026 and close 30 November 2026; please verify details on the official portal.

  • Applicants must be nationals of, and live in, a VLIR-UOS eligible country
  • Not older than 35 on 1 January of the intake year
  • Only one ICP Connect application per year, and no other scholarship for the same or another programme at the same time
  • No previous Belgian government study scholarship and no earlier enrolment at a Belgian higher education institution
  • Deadline: 30 November 2026; verify on the official portal
