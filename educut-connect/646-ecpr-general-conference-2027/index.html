ECPR General Conference 2027

European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR) · Barcelona, Spain

Deadline 8 January 2027, 23:59 (Europe/London)Deadline 8 January 2027, 23:59 (Europe/London)

In short

The ECPR General Conference 2027 takes place from 7 to 10 September 2027 at Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona, Spain. Political science researchers can submit panel and individual paper proposals online, in English, from 17 November 2026. Section chairs review proposals from 11 January 2027. Verify all details on the official portal before applying.

  • Open call for panels and papers in political science.
  • Proposals are submitted online in English to sections; section-specific rules may apply.
  • Conference: 7 to 10 September 2027, Pompeu Fabra University, Barcelona.
  • Deadline: 8 January 2027, 23:59 (Europe/London), midnight UK time.
