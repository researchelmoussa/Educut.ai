The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program

University of Cape Town · Cape Town, South Africa

Deadline 31 October 2026Deadline 31 October 2026

In short

The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Cape Town supports students for the 2027 intake. Applicants to honours, master's and eligible postgraduate diploma programmes apply by 31 October 2026, after submitting an academic application to UCT. It is aimed at postgraduate candidates planning to study in Cape Town, South Africa.

  • Scholarship programme at the University of Cape Town, South Africa, for the 2027 intake.
  • For honours, master's and eligible postgraduate diploma applicants.
  • Submit an academic application to UCT first, then the scholarship application.
  • Deadline: 31 October 2026.
  • Status is likely open: confirm the dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
