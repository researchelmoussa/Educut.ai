Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellows Program

Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society (MIDAS), University of Michigan · United States

Deadline 23 November 2026, 23:59 (America/New_York)Deadline 23 November 2026, 23:59 (America/New_York)

In short

A two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Michigan's Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society (MIDAS) for researchers applying AI in the natural sciences, engineering and mathematics, working on campus with one AI mentor and one science mentor. This is the final application cycle: the priority deadline is 23 November 2026 and fellowships start in August or September 2027. The salary is $81,000 annually (2026-27 rate) plus benefits.

  • PhD in any field, received after 1 September 2022 and conferred before the August or September 2027 start
  • Two U-M faculty mentors for the full two years (one AI, one science); a prior or current faculty mentor is ineligible
  • Biomedical proposals on disease diagnosis, prevention or treatment are out of scope; fellows work in person on campus
  • Materials include CV, research statement, Individualized Development Plan, Responsible AI statement and letters from both mentors
  • Priority deadline: 23 November 2026, 23:59 (America/New_York); confirm current status on the official page before applying
