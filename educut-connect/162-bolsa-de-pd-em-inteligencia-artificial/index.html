Bolsa de PD em Inteligência Artificial

Centro de IA e Gestão Pública (CIA-GP), Universidade de São Paulo (EACH) · São Paulo, Brazil

Deadline 15 November 2026 (America/Sao_Paulo)Deadline 15 November 2026 (America/Sao_Paulo)

In short

A FAPESP postdoctoral fellowship (one position) at the Center for AI and Public Governance (CIA-GP), Universidade de São Paulo (EACH), in São Paulo, Brazil. The research uses AI and process mining to predict, optimize and visualize regulatory workflows, under principal investigator Marcelo Fantinato. The expected start is December 2026.

  • Experience in data science, process mining and machine learning, with Python programming.
  • Apply by email to the principal investigator with PDF documents and the subject line given in the official call.
  • Documents: FAPESP-format CV, updated Lattes CV, master's and doctoral transcripts, and a motivation letter of up to 1,500 words.
  • Deadline: 15 November 2026 (America/Sao_Paulo). Likely open: confirm dates and requirements on the official page before applying.
