Graduate Admissions, Fall 2027

The American University in Cairo (AUC) · Cairo, Egypt

Deadline 15 December 2026 (Africa/Cairo)Deadline 15 December 2026 (Africa/Cairo)

In short

The American University in Cairo (AUC) is accepting graduate applications for Fall 2027, for master's and doctoral programmes in all fields. The early deadline is 15 December 2026 and the regular deadline is 1 April 2027. Fellowship cycle 1 runs from 22 December 2026 to 17 January 2027, and decisions usually follow three to four months after submission. Applications are likely open; please verify dates and details on the official portal before applying.

  • Graduate programmes in all fields, master's and doctoral level, Fall 2027 entry
  • Regular deadline: 1 April 2027; applications submitted after a deadline are evaluated at the next deadline
  • Fellowship applicants must submit the graduate application at least two weeks before the fellowship deadline
  • Fellowship cycle 1: 22 December 2026 to 17 January 2027
  • Early deadline: 15 December 2026 (Africa/Cairo)
