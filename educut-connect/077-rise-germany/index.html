RISE Germany

DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) · Germany

Deadline 30 November 2026, 23:59 (CET)Deadline 30 November 2026, 23:59 (CET)

In short

DAAD RISE Germany 2027 offers three-month summer research internships at German universities and research institutes. It is for undergraduates from North American, British and Irish universities studying biology, chemistry, computer science, physics, earth sciences or engineering. Internships start between 15 May and 15 July 2027.

  • Enrolled in a bachelor's programme at a North American, British or Irish university.
  • Field of study: biology, chemistry, computer science, physics, earth sciences, engineering or a closely related subject.
  • Three-month onsite internships in Germany starting between 15 May and 15 July 2027.
  • Applications close on 30 November 2026, 23:59 (CET); reference letters are due 15 December 2026.
  • Confirm that the call is open and check every date on the official portal before applying.
