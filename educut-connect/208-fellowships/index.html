In short

Graduate fellowships at The American University in Cairo (AUC), Egypt, for fall 2027 entry across graduate fields of study. Fellowship cycle 1 runs from 22 December 2026 to 17 January 2027, and cycle 2 runs in April to May 2027. The graduate admission application must be submitted at least two weeks before the fellowship deadline.
