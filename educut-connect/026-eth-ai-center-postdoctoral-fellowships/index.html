ETH AI Center Postdoctoral Fellowships

ETH AI Center, ETH Zurich · Zurich, Switzerland

Deadline 27 October 2026, 16:00 (Europe/Zurich)Deadline 27 October 2026, 16:00 (Europe/Zurich)

In short

The ETH AI Center at ETH Zurich invites applications for its 2027 Postdoctoral Fellowships in interdisciplinary artificial intelligence research, based in Zurich, Switzerland. The fellowships are for researchers who hold a PhD, or will complete it before the start, and want to carry out AI research across disciplines. A postdoc selection symposium is held in January 2027.

  • PhD held at the submission deadline or completed at least three months before the start.
  • At least two references are required.
  • A postdoc selection symposium is held in January 2027.
  • Deadline: 27 October 2026, 16:00 (Europe/Zurich).
