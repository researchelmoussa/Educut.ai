Scholarship for Foreign Students

Honjo International Scholarship Foundation · Japan

Deadline 31 October 2026 (Asia/Tokyo)Deadline 31 October 2026 (Asia/Tokyo)

In short

The Honjo International Scholarship Foundation offers a non-repayable monthly stipend to non-Japanese graduate students (Master and PhD) at Japanese universities, for programmes starting in April 2027. The stipend is ¥180,000 to ¥230,000 per month depending on programme length. Applications are online; interviews take place in early February 2027.

  • For non-Japanese citizens enrolled in or applying for a graduate programme in Japan starting April 2027.
  • The enrolment period must be longer than 12 months.
  • Basic Japanese conversation is needed, as interviews are held in Japanese.
  • Interviews in early February 2027; results announced on 31 March 2027.
  • Application deadline: 31 October 2026 (Asia/Tokyo).
