Undergraduate Complexity Research

Santa Fe Institute (SFI) · United States

Deadline 13 January 2027, 16:00 America/Denver (MST)Deadline 13 January 2027, 16:00 America/Denver (MST)

In short

The Santa Fe Institute's Undergraduate Complexity Research program is a full-time, ten-week, in-person research experience in the United States, from 30 May to 7 August 2027. It is open to undergraduates from all backgrounds in the physical, natural and social sciences and mathematics. Applications are due on 13 January 2027 at 4 p.m. Mountain time; the year is inferred from the 2027 program dates. Confirm on the official page.

  • Undergraduates from all backgrounds in the physical, natural and social sciences and mathematics
  • Strong quantitative skills or programming experience favourably considered
  • Full-time, in-person, ten weeks: 30 May to 7 August 2027
