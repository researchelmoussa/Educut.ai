Wallenberg-NTU Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship (2027 call)

Nanyang Technological University (NTU) · Singapore

Deadline 31 December 2026, 23:59 (Asia/Singapore)Deadline 31 December 2026, 23:59 (Asia/Singapore)

In short

NTU Singapore's Wallenberg-NTU Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship 2027 call is for researchers in any field within five years of their PhD who can start by 31 December 2027. Applicants submit a research proposal, and referee reports are due by 7 January 2027. Applications close on 31 December 2026 at 23:59 Singapore time. The terms for this call were not captured, so check the official page before applying.

  • Within five years of obtaining a PhD or equivalent degree.
  • Available to take up the position no later than 31 December 2027.
  • Submit a thoughtful and realistic research proposal; referees' reports are due by 7 January 2027.
  • Deadline: 31 December 2026, 23:59 (Asia/Singapore).
