PhD scholarship - RTP and DUPRS

Deakin University · Australia

Deadline 25 October 2026 (Australia/Melbourne)Deadline 25 October 2026 (Australia/Melbourne)

In short

Deakin University in Australia offers research degree scholarships through the Research Training Program (RTP) and the Deakin University Postgraduate Research Scholarship (DUPRS), for doctoral and research master's candidates in the February 2027 intake. This listing covers the 2027 domestic round, which opened on 6 July 2026; outcomes are expected on 23 December 2026.

  • Research degree scholarships (RTP and DUPRS) for doctoral and research master's study.
  • For the February 2027 intake.
  • This is the 2027 domestic round; the international round closed on 23 August 2026.
  • Outcomes are expected on 23 December 2026.
  • Deadline: 25 October 2026 (Australia/Melbourne).
