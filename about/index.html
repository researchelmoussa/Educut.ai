About EduCut.ai · Bridging AI with Education

About EduCut.ai

EduCut.ai is an AI training provider for companies, universities and professionals, based in Switzerland. It offers 212 courses in 18 specialized academies and a free 3-minute assessment that recommends focused AI learning paths.

Our story

EduCut AI was founded in Switzerland by high-ranking academics, senior researchers, and university professors in the domain of Artificial Intelligence from world-class research institutions.

Having spent decades at the frontier of machine learning, neural architectures, and algorithmic systems, the founding faculty observed an urgent and widening disconnect: while artificial intelligence was advancing at unprecedented velocity within scientific laboratories, enterprises and educational institutions struggled to translate raw research models into safe, sovereign, and practical operational capabilities. Conventional training was often superficial, product-biased, or rapidly obsolete, while traditional university curricula moved too slowly to meet executive and engineering demands.

EduCut AI was created to solve this challenge. Conceived as an agile bridge between frontier academic research and institutional execution, EduCut provides systematic, vendor-neutral upskilling across 18 specialized academies and 212 accredited curricula. By translating cutting-edge scientific rigor into modular, business-aligned learning paths, EduCut AI empowers organizations, researchers, and future leaders to build genuine, lasting AI capabilities.

Our approach

EduCut.ai offers AI learning built for business, not a generic course library. The catalogue is AI-only and organized around precise professional needs, from productivity and business functions to LLMs, RAG, agents, governance, security and enterprise transformation, and courses are updated and expanded as AI evolves. Instead of labelling organizations simply “beginner” or “advanced”, a short assessment combines organizational maturity, business priorities, target teams, technical ambition and governance needs into three paths of increasing depth.

What EduCut.ai offers

18 academies in five topic areas:

  • AI & data foundations: AI Foundations, Generative AI and Data Analytics
  • AI strategy & transformation: AI Transformation, AI for Leaders and AI Productivity
  • AI for business functions: AI for Marketing, AI for Finance, AI for HR and AI for Education
  • Building with LLMs: LLM Engineering, RAG Systems, AI Agents, AI for Developers and Prompt Engineering
  • Trustworthy AI: AI Governance, Responsible AI and AI Security

Faculty and experts

Our academies and curricula are authored, reviewed, and instructed by high-ranking academics, senior researchers, and university professors in the domain of Artificial Intelligence from world-class research institutions. To preserve academic neutrality and ongoing research commitments, individual faculty profiles are coordinated directly with verified client organizations during cohort planning and syllabus customization.
